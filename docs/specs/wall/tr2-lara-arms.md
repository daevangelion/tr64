# Tomb Raider II: Lara's pistols, shotgun and flares

Game and release: Tomb Raider II, Steam appid 225300, Windows PC `Tomb2.exe`, 912896 bytes, SHA-256 `fd9c91d14d54540b3e00c9bedafeef9e4ddb1e9542adc9f29170c7f19b1a657b`.
Reader: WallRE2. Date: 2026-11-22. Evidence: retail PC rules and `WALL.TR2` animation data, checked against the named [schema 20 and 21 traces](tr2-oracle-trace.md). Y increases downward; angles are signed turns of 65536 units; speeds are world units per logic tick at 30 Hz. The field names below are those of the trace contract.

The earlier [firearms rules](tr2-lara-ground-and-weapons.md#pistols-automatic-pistols-uzis-and-shotgun) give the lock, damage and reserve distinctions from I. This document gives the exact II pistol and shotgun timing. Flare handling is new in II; the [water and flare specification](tr2-water-and-flares.md) gives the lighting bands and wet rules, whose use by the arm and by the loose item is distinguished below.

## Pistol attempts and random draws (Q-T2A-01)

**Established statically — per-attempt reserve.** A fresh game's live pistol reserve (`lara.ammo[0]`, not a ring entry) is 1000. Each pistol bullet attempt that reaches firing sets the common reserve to 1000 **before** the ammunition check, then spends one unit **before** choosing its direction or testing for a hit. Both arms use that same reserve: the first attempt may begin at 1000 or 999, finishes at 999, and the second resets to 1000 and leaves 999 again. There is no idle refill. Every issued bullet increases `save.ammoUsed` by one even if it misses. The normal ready-weapon empty-reserve check is earlier, before either firing attempt; ordinary pistols do not become empty through firing. Only the resulting reserve and bullet count are visible in post-tick samples.

**Established statically — per bullet.** The **right** hand updates first and gets first use of a last single round; the left follows. An issued ray draws from the gameplay stream first for **pitch**, then for **yaw**. This corrects the earlier reading of the hand and axis order; the post-tick totals alone could not distinguish either. Each draw advances the unsigned 32-bit state by multiplying by 1103515245 and adding 12345, keeping the low 32 bits; take bits 10 through 24 of the updated state, counting from bit 0, as the 15-bit result. Subtract 16384, multiply by 1456 and divide by 65536, truncating toward zero, for the signed angle offset (−364…363). Add the pitch offset to the current arm pitch, and the yaw offset to the sum of current arm yaw and Lara's body yaw. The whole 15-bit result contributes; there is no separate hit roll. Scenery hit by the same ray can have its own effects. The record verifies the combined post-tick stream, not which hand or axis received a draw.

**Established — comparison.** `pistols` has no locked target, hit or scenery break. The two rays at each volley advance gameplay RNG four times; the other ticks leave that stream alone. A replay matches both arms, reserve, bullet statistic and RNG on all 116 samples. The RNG states below are after both bullets, starting from the fixture's 3547461959.

## Pistol arm poses (Q-T2A-02)

**Established — frame convention.** The two hands have independent counters after they are ready. They share the pistol pose bank: frames 0–4 select animation 218 (lowered to aimed), 5–12 select 219 (holster reach), 13–23 select 220 (draw/return), and 24 and up select 221 (recoil). Frame values in the trace are these arm counters, not Lara's body frame. This has one observable exception at completion of holstering: the final selected arm animation survives resetting the arm counter to 0.

| Stage | Established behaviour |
|---|---|
| Start draw from free hands | An eligible held Draw request starts drawing (`weaponState` 2); changing to an eligible selected weapon can also request its draw. Reset the arm counters, arm angles, target, aim locks and flash, then show the first draw pose, 219/5, in the same tick. Carry on with the draw if Draw is released. The normal [weapon admission rules](tr2-lara-ground-and-weapons.md#pistols-automatic-pistols-uzis-and-shotgun) still govern environment and hands. |
| Finish drawing | Advance both arms by one per tick. At 13 move both pistols to the hands and play the draw sound. Reaching 23 completes the draw, sets Ready (4) and both arms to 218/0 with no aim locks or target. Ready firing does not also run on that completion tick. |
| Ready, aim and fire | Update target and arm aim first. A locked arm stays eligible to raise and stay aimed. With Action held and no selected target, either arm may free-fire without a lock; when a target exists, a hand lacking its lock does not fire. An eligible arm advances from 0 through 4 by one per tick. A tick starting at 4 fires if Action is held, jumps that hand to 221/24, and sets flash to 3. An aimed hand can sit at 4 without firing. A positive flash counter loses one at the start of each later weapon update. |
| Recoil, with the hand still eligible | After 24 progress through 32; the next tick returns to 218/4 without firing. Continuing Action can fire again on the tick after that. If a hand no longer has a lock and is not free-firing, recoil goes straight to 218/4 on that tick, rather than finishing 221. On later ineligible ticks, 4…1 lower one step per tick. Releasing Action thus interrupts recoil in the untargeted record, but does not require interrupting a still-locked arm. |
| Draw while ready; holster priority | A held Draw request, pending weapon change or required environmental stow selects holster (3) **before** the ready-weapon update. It wins over Action, whether the arm was ready to fire or in recoil. A recoil pose becomes 218/4 on the first holster update. From frames 4…1, each arm lowers one frame and reduces each yaw and pitch by its current angle divided by that frame count, truncating toward zero. From 0 clear that arm's angles and select 220/23. |
| Return, finish and re-draw | Count 23 down toward 5; crossing to 12 returns that gun to its holster. At 5 an arm waits for the other. When both reach 5, keep the resulting 219 pose selection but store both counters as 0, clear target and locks, and set hands free (0). This final sample is 219/0, not 218/0 or “no animation”. A released Draw is not needed to finish the current motion; a still-held Draw can request a draw again on a later free update. |

## Pistol lock and creature strikes

**Established statically — acquisition.** A ready pistol update with Action released discards the old selection, then searches even with Action still released. Holding Action keeps an existing selection and searches only if it has none. To acquire, scan the five ordinary creature slots in slot order, excluding Lara and occupants with zero or negative health. The candidate origin must be strictly within 8192 of Lara's point 650 above her feet. Aim at the horizontal midpoint of the nearest authored pose bounds, turned by the candidate's yaw, and one third of the way from its top toward its bottom. Half-distance and third-distance quotients truncate toward zero; rotation products are summed at 14-bit scale then rounded down. On a keyframe tie use the earlier pose. An unobstructed line and an inclusive cone of ±10920 in both pitch and yaw, relative to Lara's body plus torso, admit the candidate. It replaces the best candidate only if strictly nearer and its absolute yaw is less than the old best absolute yaw plus 2730. Distance ties keep the earlier slot.

**Established statically — angles and retention.** Use the [integer bearing](tr2-camera.md#exact-signed-bearing) from the sight point for the horizontal yaw. For pitch only, if any of the three signed differences is outside signed 16-bit range, divide all three by four rounding down, repeatedly until all fit. Take the integer square root of squared horizontal distance; use the same bearing with that as forward distance and the vertical difference as the side. Pitch is opposite in sign to the vertical difference. For each locked arm the sight test uses these angles relative to the **body alone**. A clear line and yaw/pitch within ±10920 establish both locks; after leaving that cone, the left can retain yaw −30940…10920 and the right −10920…30940, both with pitch ±14560. Endpoints are inclusive. Neither arm can gain lock solely through its wider retention limits. Obstruction drops both locks, not the chosen item while Action is held. Each arm's yaw and pitch approach the current locked direction, or zero without lock, by up to 1820 per tick; roll is zero. The head and torso both reflect half the only locked arm's aim, or one quarter of the sum of two locked arms, with division toward zero. Weapon aim leaves them unchanged if neither hand is locked.

**Established — weapon sight (Q-T2K-05).** Acquisition and retention both start at Lara's current X/Z, 650 above her origin, with her live room as the starting room. The aim point above uses the candidate's nearest-key bounds and full world origin; the horizontal offset is rotated by its yaw only. Do not use head, hand, muzzle or interpolated pose geometry for either point.

**Established — occlusion.** Use the same margin-free, two-axis tile-boundary sight test as the camera's [sight line](tr2-camera.md#two-different-ways-to-keep-a-point-clear-q-t2c-01-q-t2c-02), not its separate navigation-box or point clearance. Weapon visibility uses the full floor and ceiling slopes, without the camera's optional steep-slope flattening. The axis with the smaller displacement is swept first, Z first on equality, then the other from Lara toward the potentially shortened endpoint. Sample both sides of each boundary at the same signed, rounded transverse coordinate and height; equality with floor or ceiling is clear. A blocked crossing or the final endpoint's floor/ceiling shortening fails the lock test; a usable clipped endpoint does not restore it. **Established statically — endpoint room.** For the final height-plane check start the endpoint's linked-room lookup in the room left by the last nonzero sweep, not necessarily the candidate's room. With both horizontal differences zero there is no sweep and the candidate's live room supplies the starting room. The published lock ticks do not distinguish these seeds. Item mesh spheres and static scenery bounds are not tested here. A door or other object that changes sector geometry can still block through that geometry; projectile collision after acquisition is a separate check.

**Established — first-lock check.** At `tiger-shoot` tick 21 the ray starts at (64518, 4984, 35417) in room 36. The tiger's nearest frame for 291/120 is 121, whose midpoint and upper-third offset put the endpoint at (62982, 5214, 28329) in room 35. The X sweep clears both crossings. In the Z sweep at (64167, 5036, 33792) the floor is 5376; the later near/far pair at Z 29696 and 29695, X 63283, Y 5168 resolves rooms 36 and 35 respectively and clears both floors. The endpoint floor and ceiling do not shorten the line. The resulting clear sight matches acquisition and both arm locks at that tick.

**Established statically — ray against a creature.** Form the ray from the same point 650 above Lara and the actual spread-adjusted angles, after her contact resolution and the arm adjustment. Consider only the selected creature. The sphere centres and radii are those of its model meshes in pose order, even if a mesh is not drawn. Use its nearest body keyframe, with the earlier key on a tie; include the authored skeleton translations and rotations and the live extra joint rotations. Express the centres in ray coordinates (positive forward, downward and right relative to the ray). The ray basis uses the signed 14-bit trigonometry of [movement](tr2-lara-ground-and-weapons.md#projecting-speed-along-a-heading-q-t2c-09): the horizontal yaw basis is tilted by the signed pitch, rounding the products down at that scale. The pose transforms keep 14-bit coefficients; scale back to world units with signed rounding down. A sphere qualifies when both absolute perpendicular offsets are below its radius, their squared sum is no greater than radius squared, and its centre is strictly farther forward than the radius. The smallest depth minus radius wins; ties keep the earlier mesh. Use that depth for the hit position. No unrelated creature is substituted. A hit does one HP of pistol damage, sets the creature's recent-hit flag and adds one to `save.ammoHits`; only damage crossing from positive to nonpositive health adds a kill. Blood does not consume gameplay RNG. The direct creature hit is not terrain-shortened at this later stage; selection and retention checked sight separately.

**Established statically — terrain result of a pistol miss (Q-T2K-10).** If there is no qualifying sphere, start at Lara's X/Z and Y minus 650 in her live room. Extend the spread-adjusted ray 8192 units, multiplying each unit-direction component by 8192 and rounding down at the signed 14-bit scale. Use the **same full-slope, margin-free linked-sector sweep as weapon visibility** above: smaller absolute horizontal difference first, Z first on equality, two samples per boundary, then the final height-plane check. The resulting endpoint and room are used even when the sweep reports blocked. There is no camera margin, slope flattening or separate ground probe.

**Established statically — scenery and effect.** Test eligible unbroken scenery items (types 72 and 88) against the ray toward the terrain result, in the rooms visited by the last nonzero sweep and in their room-item order. This is a bounds test against those items, not a test against arbitrary static meshes or incidental creatures. When eligible scenery is selected it takes precedence over the terrain ricochet. Otherwise a blocked sweep attempts one ricochet at the returned endpoint and room; a clear range-limit miss creates none. A ricochet takes one shared room-effect slot, or is refused without a draw when the pool is full. On success it draws once from the presentation stream for its initial sprite, not from gameplay RNG. Lara fires after the effect updates, so the new ricochet first updates next tick and retires on its fourth effect update. Muzzle flashes are separate and do not take room-effect slots.

**Established — terrain and clean-miss controls.** A separate effect measurement reproduced the public `pistols` and `spider-shoot` samples exactly; the public schema itself does not carry the effects. At `pistols` 76 two bullets miss a creature and both create ricochets in room 35 at (65535, 3924, 25224) and (65535, 3926, 25171); the linked sample at X 65536 is blocked. Tick 86 also creates two. Ticks 96 and 106 issue two shots each but neither creates a spark or blood: those are clear range misses in a level without either eligible breakable type. In `spider-shoot` the ricochet counts on ticks 290, 300, 310, 320, 330, 340 and 350 are 1, 1, 2, 1, 2, 1 and 1 respectively. At 290 the spark starts at (25600, −646, 35352) in room 4 with four updates left. At 350 the four occupied slots are the campfire flame, two blood effects and one new ricochet. Neither firing a bullet nor a change in presentation RNG alone proves a ricochet.

**Established statically — which joints move the spheres (Q-T2K-02).** Both shot spheres and world-space contact spheres use the same skeleton adjustments. The retail game enables extra yaw on the tiger at zero-based mesh 22 when it prepares the loaded model; the authored bone word alone does not specify it. Apply the bone's translation and its nearest key's rotation first, then apply the recorded `jointYaw` about that joint's local Y axis. This affects its sphere and descendants on that branch. The tiger consumes no other extra joint values. Its death animation eases `jointYaw` toward zero by up to 910 per update while it still has a brain; after retirement there is no extra rotation, even while the corpse is selected. The small spider (36) has no enabled extra joint rotation: it uses only the authored poses and disappears at breakup. More generally, use the loaded model's enabled axes, including any retail adjustments; after each bone's authored pose rotation the extra rotations are taken in local Y, X, Z order, skipping axes not enabled. A creature with no brain uses no extra rotations. The model setup and the shared sphere geometry establish the mapping; the shot samples do not expose individual sphere transforms.

**Established statically — body tilt and the ray (Q-T2K-03).** For either handgun arm, the ray's yaw is Lara's body yaw plus the arm's current yaw plus yaw spread. Its pitch is the arm's current pitch plus pitch spread; its roll is zero. Neither Lara's body pitch nor her body roll contributes directly to the ray basis, even in a tilted dry pose. Body pitch can still affect the separate sight and lock tests that adjust the arm. Surface or submerged mode suppresses ordinary pistol use through the [water/weapon interlock](tr2-water-and-flares.md); it is not a separate pitch convention for shots.

**Established — retaining a corpse versus losing the item.** While the chosen item exists, pistol retention checks line and angles, not health, range or active-list presence. In `tiger-shoot`, selection appears with Action released at tick 21, disappears at 36 and returns at 38. Both pistols hit at 95; from 103 only the right retains aim. The tiger reaches 0 HP and credits the kill at 275. It advances its death pose on 276 and retires from `active` at 301, leaving the corpse item selected. Hits on 305 through 395 still count but add no kill. On 401 Action release clears the choice, and the dead tiger is not eligible for reacquisition. In `spider-shoot`, actual item removal clears the choice even with Action held. Slot 10 is removed on 281, and the same ready update selects the living spider in slot 9. Removal of an item is not merely retirement from its active updates.

**Established — nearest pose and directed checks.** For sight bounds and mesh spheres, choose the later physically stored key only when the elapsed fraction is strictly more than half the [effective key interval](tr2-lara-slide-height-and-gaze.md#pose-sampling-at-a-clips-tail-q-t2c-07), not half of the nominal spacing if the interval shortened. At `tiger-shoot` 247 the tiger is in animation 286/frame 17. The next relative key is at 20, beyond absolute end 17; the one-tick tail picks key 5 (counting from zero) rather than key 4. That gives the recorded right-arm pitch −3238, not −2632 with nominal spacing. All 430 ready updates match. A separate wall-side measurement with the same public trace checked the 32 issued ray angles and their sphere decisions. At 95 the right ray is pitch −1499 and yaw −24516, then the left is −1276 and −24795. Both choose mesh 17, at distances 1076 and 1073 respectively. All 32 hit decisions reproduce from measured sphere centres; this check did not independently regenerate those centres from the mesh data.

## Shotgun draw, firing and stow

**Established — reserve and pose.** Fresh Great Wall stock includes the shotgun and 12 pellet units. Selecting the carried gun in the native ring leaves that reserve unchanged. Both arms use the same shotgun animation and the same frame measured from that animation's authored start, not from the start of the whole draw. A temporary weapon-pose item supplies this timing; its slot is `weaponItem`, distinct from both the carried ring type and the model on Lara's back. It neither collides in the level nor appears among the ticking world items. Lara's body animation is independent of the weapon pose.

**Established — slot choice.** The pose uses the ordinary reserve of free level-item slots, not a fixed shotgun slot. Fresh level state makes them available in ascending order starting immediately after the authored placements, so the first shotgun draw here uses 106. Dynamic items already allocated can take earlier slots; a released dynamic slot is made available first for the next allocation. The fresh assignment is recorded; reuse after another allocation is established statically.

**Established statically — pellet order.** Use Lara's body yaw plus the current left-arm yaw, and use the left-arm pitch without body pitch or roll. A discharge attempts six independent rays from Lara's horizontal origin, 500 above her feet. For each attempt, first draw gameplay yaw spread, then pitch spread. In both cases subtract 16384 from the 15-bit draw, multiply by 3640, divide by 65536 truncating toward zero, and add the resulting −910…909 offset. If a pellet unit is available, spend it and consume two further gameplay draws, pitch then yaw, for the ordinary ray spread. The shotgun's ordinary spread multiplier is zero, so these two draws do not change the direction. A shot that reaches all six attempts but has only some units left still consumes both outer draws for each empty attempt, but no inner draws for those empty attempts. A completely empty gun ordinarily fails the earlier ready check, not this pellet loop. A full shell spends six units and takes 24 gameplay draws, with no separate hit roll.

**Established — ray and damage comparison.** Use the [same sphere and terrain rules](#pistol-lock-and-creature-strikes) as the pistol after choosing the shotgun's origin and direction. A pellet that misses creature spheres has range 8192 and may strike breakable scenery or make a ricochet as in the pistol rule. Each issued pellet counts for `ammoUsed`, and each qualifying creature hit counts for `ammoHits` and deals 3 damage (I: 4). The six pellets resolve in order; crossing from positive to nonpositive health credits one kill, not a kill for every later pellet on the same body. In `shotgun-tiger`, tick 64 counts six fired and four hits (tiger 20 to 8, reserve 12 to 6); tick 97 adds six fired and six hits (tiger 8 to −10, reserve 6 to 0). The two ticks each consume exactly 24 gameplay draws, leaving states 802531111 and 1884979983, respectively. Against the first shell's geometry, the four-hit outcome distinguishes yaw before pitch; the totals alone cannot establish the otherwise unused inner draws. **Static-only scope:** part shells, further shells after a replenished reserve, save or reload of a spent reserve, other targets, and selecting the shotgun with a lit flare are not played here. Neither bonus stock, shallows, changing guns during draw nor manual pump interruption is exercised.

**Established — two pickups in one commit.** In `shotgun-ammo`, Action at (17536, −256, 37252) starts the dry pickup motion at the shared location of the room-42 shotgun box (item 29, type 143) and large medikit (item 28, type 150). Neither addition inserts a new ring entry. The [ordinary pickup rule](tr2-triggers-and-interaction.md#pickups) permits the two effects without a second animation or Action press.

## Held flare: selection, lighting and overlay (Q-T2A-03)

**Established — control and counters.** The Flare control is a held test, not a separately detected rising edge. In a free-hands update with another weapon selected, it requests a flare if one is in inventory. With guns ready it can request a flare and start stowing the guns first. ## Letting go and the loose item's life (Q-T2A-04)

### Manual toss versus dropping to draw a gun

**Established — standing throw.** When Lara is unmounted, aiming to remain standing and in idle stand clip 103, stowing replaces the pose with body clip 189, at its first authored frame plus the current arm phase (5363 plus that phase in The Great Wall). The body supplies the throw pose, so the independent arm overlay is off. `flareFrame` stores the absolute body motion counter and is advanced after a continuing stow update, one ahead of the body sample. When that counter is already at or beyond the first frame plus 31, the stow ends: restore the remembered gun, clear the arm counter without replacing its last animation selection, and restore Stand clip 11/frame 185, also retained in `flareFrame`. If the clip begins with an arm phase already beyond the threshold, this finish can happen immediately, without a throw. A separate press at tick 130, during the idle flourish (arm phase 99 on the preceding tick), returned to Stand with arm 258/0 and no loose flare.

**Established — where in the arm motion.** Arm phase 0 enters the throw at 1. Without that whole-body finish, an arm already in the 72–93 or 95–109 band advances to that band's end before beginning at 1. Reaching arm phase 21 creates the thrown item and removes the hand mesh. Frames 1–20 light the held flare; the spawn tick adds no held age. In an arm-only throw, reaching 33 returns hands to free and the remembered gun, clears overlay and target, and zeroes the arm and retained body counters. A standing body finish takes priority if it occurs, including a stow that began in another state and reaches idle Stand later. A continuous Flare hold from tick 36 corroborates both parts: it begins an arm-only throw in the slide at 98 without another press, releases at 118, then uses the standing finish at 129 and starts drawing the second flare at 130. Switching directly to an eligible gun is a drop, not a trip through the throw frames.

### Release-hand transform

**Established — exact body key.** Start with Lara's orientation and the selected body's root translation and rotation. Follow the authored torso translation and rotation, including her live torso turn. For the arm, use the **left** shoulder, elbow and hand translations. Use left upper-arm, forearm and hand rotations, from the independent left arm if its overlay is active and otherwise from the body key. Apply the local point in the resulting hand frame and add Lara's world origin, rounding each transformed offset down from its 14 fractional bits. `flare-throw` 167 uses body 189/5383, exactly a key at spacing 2, with the overlay off; this gives (64108, 4659, 26224).

**Established — between body keys.** Form the transforms through the torso for both body keys, including the live torso turn on each. Blend each matrix component using the [ordinary body fraction](tr2-lara-slide-height-and-gaze.md#pose-sampling-at-a-clips-tail-q-t2c-07), truncating the fractional change toward zero; with divisor 2 use the two-component mean rounded down. From that blended torso, the flare hand uses the authored **right shoulder translation**, but still the **left** upper-arm rotation and the **left** elbow and hand translations and rotations. With the overlay active those three rotations come from the left arm's exact pose at `armL.frame` in its authored arm range (spacing 1 in this level); they are not interpolated with either body key or headed by an extra weapon-aim rotation. Without the overlay the rotations come from the preceding body key's left arm, also not blended separately. Finish with the same hand-point and origin rounding as an exact body key. The right-shoulder offset is a consequence of the fractional **body** sample, not a distinct throw or drop option. `flare-drop` 147 uses body 103/1629, one tick into its four-tick interval, and the held 254/0 arm. The right translation gives X 64172, whereas taking the left translation there would give 64056; Y and Z match at 4742 and 26192.
