## 1. New Lara rules shared by the two levels

### 1.1 Climbing, ledges and reversal

Wall climbing is not I's ledge grab. Floor data function 6 marks climbable faces independently: +Z = 1, +X = 2, −Z = 4, −X = 8. Only the marked faces are climbable. The wall states are stance (56), up (57), left (58), climb over the top (59), right (60) and down (61). The manual and route establish the following visible behaviour:

| Situation | Player action and result |
|---|---|
| At the foot of a ladder, hands available | Face it, hold Action and press Forward to catch hold. |
| Airborne facing a ladder | Hold Action to catch the climbable wall itself, even partway up rather than only on the top edge; this works after a jump or a short drop. |
| Hanging on the wall | Keep holding Action. Forward/Back climb up/down; Left/Right move laterally where that face permits it. At a suitable top edge, Forward pulls Lara over. |
| Leaving the wall | Release Action to fall, press Jump to dismount in a backward flip, or finish climbing up onto land. A brief release and re-grab permits a faster descent. |
| Ordinary non-ladder ledge | Still grab by holding Action, shimmy along the ledge and pull over. The Great Wall flooded blade room uses an ordinary crevice traverse, not a ladder climb. |
| Forward or backward jump, just after takeoff | Roll or the opposite direction requests an aerial somersault/turn; Lara can land facing the other way. The classic controls also show use after a ladder dismount, to face and grab another wall. |

Hand clearances, allowable facing, sector-edge handling, displacement per ladder cycle, drop/re-grab timing, aerial roll windows and any fallback into ordinary ledge hang are not established by this source. After a jump collision with less than 762 units between floor and ceiling at her middle, Lara recoils in the opposite direction at one-quarter speed, in fast fall at the second frame of animation 32. She descends at no less than 1 unit per tick. An optional variant skips this low-ceiling response; that bypass is not established as original retail behaviour.

### 1.2 Wade, swim, surface and climb out

Feet depth is the vertical distance down from the waterline to Lara's origin; pool depth is the total water column at that location. The new thresholds refer to different measurements. They do not apply while she is on a vehicle or in a special scripted animation. The transitions are checked before her movement for the tick.

| Starting state and trigger | Result |
|---|---|
| Dry; waterline defined; feet more than 384 below it, total depth at most 474 | Wade; if grounded she first aims to stop. Exactly 384 in this branch does not enter Wade. |
| Dry; feet at least 384 below waterline, pool deeper than 474, her room is a water room | The dive starts 100 lower, in the room at that depth, with a splash, full air (1,800), no gravity, and head and torso facing straight ahead. Swan and fast dives enter at twice their old speed, pitched −45° and −85° respectively. Other entries use animation 112, pitch −45°, and one and a half times their old speed. |
| Wading; feet depth below 384 | Lara is back on land. If she is still in the wading animation, her next goal is Run. The wading view has elevation −22°; the manual describes wading as slower movement with a vertical rather than directional jump. |
| Wading; feet depth above 730 | She begins at the waterline plus 1, upright and looking straight ahead, without gravity, residual swim speed, or accumulated dive hold. Room membership is based on a point 381 above her feet. Backward steps lead into backward swim (140), sidesteps into lateral surface strokes (left 143, right 144), and other approaches into forward surface swim (116). |
| Submerged; leaves water room and water depth is defined; absolute distance to waterline less than 256 | She treads upright in animation 114 at the waterline plus 1, looking straight ahead, with no residual swim speed. The room is determined 381 above her feet. The dive-hold count is already 11 on entry, and the transition has breath sound 36. Nearness to the waterline is not enough while still in the water room. |
| Submerged; leaves water room, and depth undefined or waterline at least 256 away | She is airborne in forward-jump animation 34, with level pitch and roll and neutral head and torso turns at the transition. Her horizontal speed is one quarter of her former swim speed. She is under gravity, initially with zero descent speed. |
| Surface; leaves a water room with feet depth at most 384 (including no surface) | Same airborne exit as above, with horizontal speed one quarter of her former surface swim speed. |
| Surface; leaves a water room with feet depth still above 384 | She stands to wade through animation 103, upright and looking straight ahead, without residual swim speed. This is not a forward fall. |

Submerged swim accelerates by 8 per tick to 200. Forward/Back change the nose angle by 2° per tick in opposite directions. Left/Right build the yaw rate by 2.25° per tick, limited to 6° either way, and bank 3° into the turn before later corrections. Release Jump to glide. Roll requests the underwater reversal immediately from normal swimming (state 66, first frame of animation 203), without doing that tick's normal stroke acceleration. Death overrides that request. An ordinary splash makes up to ten sprites at the waterline, each starting with an independent heading and outward speed from 0 to 127. The checked PC rules produce no separate splash for wading. Air follows the values in the delta map; water pickups and levers still use Action, and Action at the right ledge permits climbing out. Exact swim/ledge clearances, glide and tread acceleration, roll completion and timing, surface dive delay, and the current and water-out collision changes against I are not established by this source.

### 1.3 Flares, weapon fire and pickups

- A new game has two loose flares. A flare box gives six; one loose flare gives one. Flare can be chosen from the ring or ignited via the Flare control. A lit flare is held in the left hand and follows Lara through movement, climbing and swimming. A repeat press throws it forward; burnout or drawing a gun drops it. The manual's “a minute or so” is a player description, not an exact lifetime. Exact charge decrement, allowable draw/throw states and frames, light radius/intensity and fuse bands, recoverable age, and ground/water flight and bounce values are not established by this source. The held flare and flying flare are distinct; a flying one is a separate object with its own orientation and flame rendering. The save restores active world flares with their age, position, room and speeds rather than relighting from scratch.
- Lara starts with holstered pistols selected and a shotgun with two shells, plus one pack of each size. Great Wall optional pickups introduce automatic pistol ammunition and the grenade launcher. Venice can provide the actual automatic pistols plus ammo for them, Uzi and M16 ammo, more shotgun shells, and medikits. A gun she already owns is converted to its ammo. Ammo without the weapon stays in loose bundles until she acquires it; exact stock is in the delta map. Health is 1,000; medikits still add 500 or 1,000, never above 1,000.
- Hold Action to keep firing toward the selected target; a loss of line of sight can break arm lock without assigning a different target. Releasing Action allows a new target. Pistols do 1 per hit, automatic pistols 2; nominal target range is 8,192 for both. A shotgun blast comprises six pellets, individually scattered up to about 5° on each aim axis, for 3 each (I: 4). Muzzle height is 500. A grenade starts at 200, slows by 1 per tick and begins to pick up downward speed at 1 per tick once already below 190. It may shatter a breakable window en route. Impact deals 30 to an eligible active creature and can break up a creature killed by it; the 512 expanded hit area is tied to striking world geometry, not a standing aura.
- The normal ground and water pickup inputs, and the keyhole ring selection, have the visible pattern of I. Do not reuse I's absolute commit frames or alignment from the first game's animation data: II's exact tolerances, pickup frames, including the loose flare animation (state 67), and item insertion points are not established by this source. A collected ordinary pickup is removed from the drawn room list on reload. Enemy-carried pickups are a new mechanism: the dying creature can release several already-authored items at its final position rather than creating a reward based solely on its type. That is essential for Venice's keys and weapon.

### 2.3 Hazards and crossing

**Moving spiked wall (85).** While triggered and not yet disabled, the wall advances 16 units per tick in its facing direction, including across rooms as long as the floor at the next position is exactly level with the wall's origin. The first different height or missing floor leaves it disabled at its last level position. Sound 204 plays when it tries to move, including on the stopping tick. Lara loses 20 health on every tick of touch while the wall can still react, even if it is not moving or its trigger is off. Each hit marks her as hit, produces three blood effects 512 above her feet, and plays sound 205. This is neither I's stationary spikes nor its rolling block. The authored start and end positions and which triggers, if any, the corridor's walls and doors share are not established by this source.

**Guillotine blades (81).** The fixed blade starts at the first frame of animation offset 2, current state 1. Triggering it from state 1 asks for the cutting state 2. In the cut, its next goal is state 1, so an extended trigger can repeat the cycle at the animation's permitted change frames. Only contact with mesh 1 while in state 2 hurts: 100 per tick, with a hit mark and two blood effects at Lara's horizontal position, 256 above the blade's origin. This is not damage from contact with the entire pedestal.

**Rolling/spinning blade (80).** Triggering turns on a rolling state (2). Use the blade animation's motion, not a fixed 16-unit wall speed. During that state it tests 1,536 forward along its heading. Missing floor there requests the turnaround state (1); the blade turns 180° when the animation actually enters that state, not on the first failed probe. The running blade plays sound 231 and contact costs 100 per tick, with a hit mark and two blood effects 512 above Lara's feet. It follows the floor height and can change rooms. The blade's own speed curve and the length of its reversal come from the placed animation data, not the lookahead distance.

**Flying discs.** Not the cutting pedestals or the two large floor-following blades. Their placement establishes a horizontal flight lane dangerous at water level but avoidable by hanging high on the left crevice and swimming underneath. Object-number assignment, reversal gates, damage, motion and collision geometry are not established by this source. Do not use one of the 100-point rules above merely because all three props look like blades.

**Boulders, stationary spikes, trapdoors and collapsible floors.** I already has all four object ideas. The Great Wall requires two boulders which activate behind Lara, can pass on either side of her and stop before the corridor ends; the approach view may be dismissed with Look. Collapsible tiles are initially traversable, give way under Lara and expose a drop; safe traversal can depend on crossing before they fall, or intentionally dropping through. The spikes under several tiles do not advance. Public classic controls confirm walking rather than running through stationary spikes can avoid incidental injury; falling onto them is unsafe. The first guardhouse's hinged trapdoor is a separate object that opens to allow passage down into water. II's exact boulder probe, reset and damage, stationary spike death threshold, collapse delay, and the precise heights at which trapdoors and breakaway tiles support or block Lara are not established by this source. A shut door and a pushable block have separate roles. A resting pushable's floor is one sector (1,024) above the original floor and is rebuilt after load. Exact II push/pull acceptance and animation frames are not established by this source.

**Campfire.** Within the ordinary 600 near test Lara loses 5 health per tick (I: 3). Less than 450 away horizontally also ignites her (I: 300) and that fire emitter stops dealing heat for 100 ticks. Attached ordinary fire subtracts 7 per tick (I: 5), follows her joint, and will not attach a second instance while the burn is already present. The attached flame goes out at its own waterline crossing, not merely because Lara's feet touch water. No timeout. Sounds and effect presentation don't replace the actual health loss. Lava-floor special deaths support multiple simultaneous flames but are not a Great Wall campfire rule.

**Zip line (102).** The player grips the starting handle with Action, rides the line across the cavern while holding it, and falls away on release. The documented main crossing uses this ride (Lara state 70), rather than an ordinary jump; the classic route releases over the far slope shortly before the end. Mount bounds, exact snap point, travel acceleration, end sensor, automatic release, room changes, whether and when it resets, and reload while riding are not established by this source.

## 3. Venice

### 3.1 Route dependencies and objects

| Stage | Objects and observed dependencies |
|---|---|
| First canal | Dogs, balcony gunman and club thug. Lara can swim under the boathouse door before unlocking it. The first button, inside the boathouse, opens access to the small wooden building across the canal. That building has two more buttons, one for each trapdoor, and leads by ladder and roof to the villa. Villa windows are breakable, and awnings have standard grab surfaces. The first balcony gunman drops the Boathouse Key for the lock inside the boathouse. Pull switches open access and the fence gate on the canal. A gunman at the dock then brings the automatic pistols. The public route explicitly names three distinct keys (Boathouse, Steel and Iron); don't infer the authored key-slot numbers from the published pickup tally or the order the player takes them. ### 3.3 Speedboat (14) and gondola (24)

The rider replaces ordinary on-foot movement. Unoccupied and occupied must be distinct; an empty hull can still reach a mine. On-foot boarding is Action from the side; the classic controls also show boarding from water with Forward and Action or landing onto the boat from above. Once aboard, Forward drives, Back slows forward motion and then reverses, Left/Right steer. Walk reduces speed without providing the on-foot safety stop at an edge. Action gives a boost and makes hard crashes dangerous. Jump with Left or Right dismounts to that side, into water or onto a usable ledge. Gates, other boats, shallows, ramp clearances and the boat lock all act on the hull; some terrain can be left behind during a jump. The player can leave one boat and use the second without taking the first boat's movement or damage state with her.

A visible wake can come from three sprite positions 700 behind the boat, with −300/0/+300 lateral offsets and outward/straight/outward headings. They start at boat height, last up to 20 ticks and have one quarter of its forward speed. This is presentation only; it does not provide buoyancy. Saved boat data retains its own motion rather than reconstructing from Lara's position. Exact eligibility bounds and mount/dismount frames, lateral occupancy checks, acceleration and cap in each mode, turn rates and lean, water heights and fall integration, hull-floor and hull-wall probes, impact thresholds and damage, empty-hull drag, wake scheduling and driver camera are not established by this source.

A gondola obstructs the route until rammed. In its idle state (1), a request for breakage (2) shatters its upper four meshes into harmless pieces. Once it reaches sinking state 3 it drops 50 per tick until the floor, then settles in state 4. The animation determines the changes; the gondola stops updating when disabled. The contact speed or position that requests the breakage, other permitted causes and whether its remaining pieces obstruct the boat are not established by this source. Test the observed case of the boat ramming a row of gondolas, not just projectile impact on them.

### 3.4 Windows, doors, lock and exit countdown

**Breakable window.** In Venice it can be shot before Lara passes through; the speedboat can also break the elevated panes as part of its ramp jump. A player grenade may shatter the ordinary breakable type (72) at contact without expiring just from that glass. Once broken, the saved ordinary pane keeps its broken state; on load the first two pane types (72 and 73) display only mesh 8 of the broken model. Decorative glass or a model with several panes is not evidence that every pane is shootable. Exact shot intersection, touch and impact thresholds, all mesh/portal changes, and boat shatter eligibility are not established by this source.

**Switches, keyholes and gates.** Pull switches, press buttons and interact with levers while underwater using Action; use the item ring for the matching key. Separate the motion animation from the rule that decides when the path is open. The lower lock's switch closes the doors and changes the water level; the second (submerged) lever opens its upper outlet afterward. The lower and upper route remain separated until both operations are complete. A fixed view can show a gate opening; Look restores the player view in the observed sequence. Very different gates can be simultaneously open or closed on the same canal route. General II switch settle frames, key trigger hand-release conditions, path block transitions (including alternate rooms), updated trigger countdowns and exact flip-effect sequencing are not established by this source.

**Exit timer.** The classic [race scene](https://tombraiders.net/stella/walks/TR2-classic/details/venice10.html) demonstrates two separate events: pressing the button opens the gate and shows a brief fixed camera; crossing the wooden threshold on the way out starts the bell and the countdown to closing the gate. There is about 23 seconds of travel time in the classic route, not an exact logic count. Swimming out below the threshold bypasses that countdown in the documented classic route. The minefield is independent: setting off the mines does not open the exit. Exact authored timer word, threshold placement/activation class, bell cadence and door reset are not established by this source. Do not start a generic timed-switch timer at the moment the button is pressed and assume the race is equivalent.

### 3.5 Boat mines and the distinct detonator

**First blast (mine 222).** A speedboat can start the chain only when it is in the room found 2,048 above the mine and less than 512 away horizontally. There is no separate vertical distance limit within that room, and swimmers cannot start the chain. The blast shatters and destroys the qualifying boat, and the mine's sector fires a heavy trigger. A rider still aboard shatters, loses all health and disappears. Someone already off the boat is not killed just for being nearby, which makes the classic [sacrifice-one-boat route](https://tombraiders.net/stella/walks/TR2-classic/details/venice7.html) possible.

**Chain blasts.** Once the chain starts, each remaining active, unexploded mine has a 1/16 chance per tick to detonate, using the logic random stream. It can do so without a boat, and does not apply the first blast's hull and rider kill.

**Spent mine.** Every blast has a splash and sound 105, plus an explosion 1,024 above the mine if there is a free effect slot. Afterward the mine shows only mesh 0, is non-collidable and cannot detonate again. Individual mines stay spent across a save and load. Whether reloading mid-chain resumes detonation of the other mines is not established by this source.

**Detonator.** Not the Venice exit button and not how the classic Venice mines are normally triggered. The separate keyed plunger is placed in [Bartoli's Hideout](https://tombraiders.net/stella/walks/TR2-classic/03bartoli.html); inserting the Detonator Key there blows open an abandoned building, loses access to its pre-blast secret route, and brings a gunman into the encounter. Exact action bounds, use frame, effect timer, damage and post-blast collision rule are not established by this source. No Venice placement of that plunger is evidenced; don't make it a new requirement to unlock the Venice exit.
