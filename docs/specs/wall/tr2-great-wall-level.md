Evidence: static reading of the retail executable compared with the authored PC level, animation, camera and campaign records. No playthrough, save round-trip or PlayStation parity is asserted.
Authored inputs: `TOMBPC.DAT`, `WALL.TR2`, `CUT1.TR2`, and `BOAT.TR2`.

Tile pairs below are global X/Z indices, 1024 units per tile; world Y increases downward. Item numbers identify placed instances, not object types. See [shared triggers and interactions](tr2-triggers-and-interaction.md), [wall climbing](tr2-climbing.md), [hazards and zip line](tr2-great-wall-objects.md), [creatures](tr2-great-wall-creatures.md) and [water and flares](tr2-water-and-flares.md) for their movement, condition and interaction rules. In the tables, “ordinary” means the normal Lara trigger, not a grounded pad. A full mask is all five bits (31).

## Loadout, dragons and reward

| Situation | Stock and commitment |
|---|---|
| Fresh Great Wall loadout | **Established.** Lara begins at 1000 health, with pistols selected and holstered, unlimited pistol ammunition, a shotgun with two shells (12 pellet units), two flares in inventory, one small and one large medikit. Unlike I's normal opening, II starts with the shotgun and flares. Initial stock, the count consumed per shotgun discharge, and the next-level inventory conversion distinguish two shells from twelve complete shots. |
| First or second distinct dragon | **Established.** Gold (type 190, item 105, room 79 tile (84,72)), Jade (191, item 77, room 54 (40,59)), and Silver (192, item 21, room 38 (62,35)) each set their own bit on acquisition. A dragon chimes on acquisition (scripted track 47) and disappears like an ordinary pickup; the normal dry commit is animation 135 at +42, authored frame 3443. Merely visiting the tile sets no bit, and neither a floor-secret action nor the Gold Dragon's separate pickup-trigger evaluation accounts for the chime. This is different from I's floor-secret action and illustrated by two dragons with no pickup action and the one that has a wake action. |
| Third distinct dragon, in any order | **Established.** The last one commits the same chime and bit, and gives the scripted bundle on that acquisition tick: grenade launcher, two grenade packs (two per pack) and one small medikit. If Lara lacks a launcher, it includes two grenades with the weapon, plus the two packs, for six new grenades; any loose grenade packs she already carried become usable at two each and any remaining placed launcher becomes grenades. If Lara already has the launcher, the script's launcher entry does not add a second launcher or another pair of grenades; the two packs give four grenades. The two inventory outcomes and the four authored grants distinguish this from generic duplicate gun pickup conversion. |
| Saving the collection and reward | **Established.** A save retains dragon bits, the disappearance of the placed dragons, and inventory quantities. A collected dragon does not reappear to set a bit on reload; during a visit the reward is exhausted when applied. The difference between a partially collected set and the already complete saved set is carried by the recorded bits and pickup status, not by changing the authored three locations. **Inferred:** a save made after the second dragon can complete the same grant from the remaining dragon, whereas a save made after the third cannot grant it again. A round-trip was not played. |
| Early ammunition pack units | **Established.** For an owned weapon: automatic pistol pack 40, Uzi pack 80, shotgun box 12 pellet units (two shells), harpoons 3, M16 pack 40, grenade pack 2. For a missing weapon, the inventory holds bundles until acquisition; the next-level stock retains equivalent quantities. The inventory acquisition and level-carry arithmetic independently agree on the unit sizes. The pistol reserve is unlimited rather than a collectable pack. |
| Venice's bonus versus The Great Wall's | **Established.** Venice's three-dragon set grants four automatic-pistol ammo packs, worth 160 rounds if she owns the gun. Unlike the four Great Wall grants, the four Venice grants are all ammunition; none supplies the actual gun. The distinct level rewards and the ammo pack quantity corroborate the contrast. |
| What is optional | **Established.** The Great Wall finish actions test neither secret count nor inventory stock, and the campaign awards the three-dragon grant on collection, not at the start. The finish records and campaign reward entries are independent. Neither establishes how much ammunition, health or light a particular route needs. |

| Skip or quit the cut | **Established.** An application quit is handled first and does not continue into Venice. Otherwise the cut checks held Action, then held Inventory if Action is not down; neither needs a newly pressed edge. By default both Left Control and Right Control count as Action, and Escape counts as Inventory; remapping changes which keys or buttons supply those controls. With Inventory suppressed by the scripted camera state, holding Escape alone does not skip, but held Action still does. Either permitted skip leads to statistics just as the last authored frame does. General menu Confirm is not a separate skip. |
| Statistics to next playable level | **Established statically.** Dismissing The Great Wall statistics leads to Venice, level 2, with Lara at Venice's own start in room 0 at (58880, −2304, 22016), facing −Z. Dragon bits are per level, as are key slots; previous dragons and keys do not count anew. The campaign's inventory rules, absence of a strip grant in this sequence, and Venice's separate spawn and reward data distinguish inventory from pose and local objectives. The observations below confirm this for the played stock and posture, not for arbitrary media or a save between stages. |

**Established — separate reader-side native observations, outside the public trace.** In each launch the cut reached its own controls, accepted Action on the third control poll, and gave way to The Great Wall statistics.
