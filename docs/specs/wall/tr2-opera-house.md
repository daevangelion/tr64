## 5. Swinging pendulum hazards (`type 58` Swinging Sandbag and `type 96` Swinging Crate/Box)

**Established (static) — complete behavioural equivalence.** Types `58` and `96` share identical per-tick updates, identical solid collision, identical shadow size (`128`), and identical animation/flag persistence. They differ only in their authored meshes and animation sound-frame placement.

| Aspect | Established retail PC behaviour |
|---|---|
| Activation and non-stopping loop | **Established (static).** Starts inactive until activated by a trigger (or immediately active if reverse-masked `0x3e00`). Once active, its per-tick update **never tests trigger state**: antitriggers or expired timers cannot stop a swinging pendulum, and it loops indefinitely. |
| Collision (solid push-out in all states) | **Established (static).** Both `58` and `96` use **ordinary solid object collision** (bounds overlap, per-mesh sphere overlap populating the per-mesh touch bitmask, and push-out) in **both** inactive and active states — **not** non-pushing trap collision. Because ordinary item animation clears the per-mesh touch bitmask on every update and Lara's collision pass runs after active items, a touch recorded on tick `N` is evaluated on tick `N + 1`. |
| Damage and blood spawn | **Established (static).** On each active tick, before advancing animation, if **any** bit of the per-mesh touch bitmask is nonzero: subtracts **`50` HP** from Lara (with no cooldown across consecutive overlapping ticks), sets Lara's hit-status flag, and spawns **`1` blood splat** in Lara's room using four gameplay random draws (`r1..r4`, uniform in `0..32767`, integer divisions truncated toward zero): X `= Lara.X + trunc((r1 − 16384) / 256)`, Y `= Lara.Y − trunc(r3 / 44)`, Z `= Lara.Z + trunc((r2 − 16384) / 256)`, speed `= Lara.speed`, yaw `= Lara.yaw + trunc((r4 − 16384) / 8)`. |
| Floor refresh and animation | **Established (static).** On each active tick, queries floor height at `(item.X, item.Y, item.Z)` in its own room (updating its recorded floor without changing rooms) and advances ordinary animation. |
| Authored clips, sound `218`, and placements in `OPERA.TR2` | **Established (static).** Both models have 3 meshes and a single looping 59-tick clip (state `1`, frames `0..58`, chaining to frame `0`): `type 58` (clip `391`) plays **sound `218`** at **frame `41`**; `type 96` (clip `422`) plays **sound `218`** at **frame `1`**. Placed in `OPERA.TR2`: three `type 58` sandbags (items `94`, `95` in room `89`; `122` in room `113`) and six `type 96` crates (item `10` in room `2`, `36` in room `35`, and `148..151` in room `123`), all activated by untimed ordinary triggers. |

## Scope limits and open edge cases

- **Static PC evidence only:** All rules, numeric bounds, animation clip frames, sound IDs, damage values, and script records above are established statically from the pinned retail PC executable, `OPERA.TR2`, and `TOMBPC.DAT`. No live playthrough capture of Opera House, mid-elevator or mid-puzzle save/load round-trip test, or PlayStation parity check was performed.
- **Grenade Launcher underwater/wade firing edge case:** In the long-gun ready pose, submerged water status (`1`) redirects to the underwater pose (`6`/`8`, which has no grenade launch branch), whereas wading water status (`4`) permits drawing and firing up to the weapon's `500`-unit wade depth limit; firing while transitioning directly between wade and deep water was not tested dynamically.
- **Elevator save/load mid-motion:** Saving and reloading while the elevator is between stops preserves current height, starting height `Y0`, wait counter, animation, and status flags; whether Lara standing inside a moving cab can clip out on a mid-motion reload was not tested dynamically.
