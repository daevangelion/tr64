# Tomb Raider II: ground movement, doors and early firearms

Game and release: Tomb Raider II, Steam appid 225300, Windows PC `Tomb2.exe`, 912896 bytes, SHA-256 `fd9c91d14d54540b3e00c9bedafeef9e4ddb1e9542adc9f29170c7f19b1a657b`.
Reader: WallRE2. Date: 2026-11-21. Inputs: executable, authored `WALL.TR2` and `BOAT.TR2`, and the Tomb Raider I wall baseline at `cdfb71d`.
Evidence: static retail rules compared with authored movement, weapon and door clips; the heading calculation is also checked across four published PC fixtures. No console parity asserted.

Distances are in game units, Y down; a sector is 1024 and a click 256. Each logic tick is 1/30 s. A full turn is 65536 angle units. Speeds are per tick; a clip's fixed-point speed and acceleration apply before the integer movement. For marked faces use [wall climbing](tr2-climbing.md); for water movement and currents use [water and flares](tr2-water-and-flares.md). The I [Lara baseline](lara-and-enemies.md) does not enumerate ordinary gait clips: the PC clip numbers here specify II, not an asserted difference in every underlying dry constant.

### Sidestep and standing turn (Q-T2C-10)

**Established — independent controls.** Normal PC input cancels opposed ordinary Left and Right bits; this does not cancel either dedicated sidestep. A dedicated step and a single ordinary turn direction can coexist, even opposite each other. Dedicated bits choose a sideways gait, ordinary direction bits can steer it. From Stand, if no higher-priority action or locomotion takes over, left sidestep wins over right sidestep, and either dedicated step wins over requesting an ordinary turn. The fixture convention Walk plus a single side direction makes a dedicated step and removes that ordinary direction; a dedicated bit plus raw Left or Right is not this shortcut.

| Starting state | Established request before the common turn-rate damping |
|---|---|
| Right or left step (21 or 22), alive | Left subtracts 409 with a limit of −728; Right adds 409 with a limit of +728. Neither ordinary turn direction means no new acceleration. Releasing the step bit for the current side requests Stand, but ordinary steering still acts until the state leaves the step; a requested Stand is not yet the end of the clip. An excluded Look leaves those turn directions available. |
| Turn right or left (6 or 7), alive | Add +409 for the right state or −409 for the left state **even if the corresponding turn direction is no longer available**. Ready weapons request fast turn; otherwise a rate past 728 in that direction requests fast turn unless Walk is held, in which case cap at 728 in that direction. Forward can select the next forward gait. Without Forward, an absent matching raw turn direction then requests Stand; this does not undo the acceleration for the current update. |
| Fast turn (20), alive | Request rate −1456 if the stored rate is negative, otherwise +1456. If the matching turn direction is absent, request Stand after setting that rate. No such acceleration is applied in these states when health is zero or below. |

**Established — damping and Look timing.** For each of these states reduce the requested rate toward zero by up to 364, then add the resulting signed turn to the body yaw. This rate is what the trace stores. Manual Look consumes the ordinary direction before the state acts when yaw Look is permitted. The turn state can still add its own last increment, and stored rate can still give a turn after damping in states that added nothing. A fresh Stand with rate zero does not add a body turn merely because its accepted Look turned the head.

**Established — route check.** Beginning from `look-sidestep` tick 149, all 62 updates through 211 reproduce state, goal, body yaw, stored turn rate and head/torso angles. Dropping the dedicated bit requests Stand, but Right still gives 364 per tick through the sidestep exit at 175. At 176 the carried Look denial leaves Right available to Stand, which requests state 6 without accelerating; damping erases the remaining rate. At 177 permitted Look consumes Right and gives +364 head and torso yaw. The still-current state 6 then adds 409, damps to 45, turns the body to 6397 and returns to Stand. Only the subsequent collision test uses body yaw plus a quarter-turn for right or minus a quarter-turn for left. Its leading sample is one body radius (100) away on the nearest cardinal axis to that heading, plus the signed sine or cosine projection of the radius on the other axis. It also samples at the origin and at both leading corners (100 ahead and 100 to either side on those cardinal axes). Resolve the sectors at 922 above the **proposed** origin (762 body height plus 160), rather than at feet height or a point 256 ahead. ### Projecting speed along a heading (Q-T2C-09)

**Established — the angle table.** A heading of zero is +Z and a positive quarter-turn is +X. Sine and cosine use the same nonnegative quarter-wave with 1025 entries, one every 16 turn units including both endpoints (0 and 16384). Each entry is the nearest integer to 16384 times the sine at that entry's angle; checking all stored entries gives that result. The values at zero and a quarter-turn are 0 and 16384.

**Established — fold first, then pick an entry.** For sine, wrap the heading to 0…65535 without rounding it to a table step. In the upper half (32768…65535), remember a negative result and subtract 32768. In either resulting half-turn, reflect phases above 16384 about the quarter-turn: the folded phase is 32768 minus the phase there. Divide the folded phase by 16, rounding down, and apply the remembered sign to the chosen entry. For cosine, add exactly 16384 to the original heading before wrapping and folding, then use that same sine rule. The quadrant mapping is therefore ascending positive, descending positive, ascending negative magnitude and descending negative magnitude, in order around the sine cycle. Do not round the original heading or a quarter-turn complement before folding.

**Established — movement and scope.** X advances by speed times signed sine and Z by speed times signed cosine. Divide each signed product by 16384 separately and round **down** (a signed arithmetic shift by 14, not a division toward zero). Lara's ordinary movement uses her live travel angle, which can differ from her body yaw; generic animated items use their current yaw. Creatures that take the ordinary animated movement step use the same item projection. This rule is for that horizontal speed step, not for a separately authored pose displacement, a collision correction, an attachment or a custom mover with an explicit single-axis update. A landing tick can take two ordinary projections with different speeds and angles, before and after the landing transition.

**Established — the distinguishing step.** In both `fall-damage` and `hard-landing` tick 37, heading 2088 and speed 50 start at X 63023, Z 23827. Sine uses phase 2080 and value 3246. Cosine adds the quarter-turn before folding; the folded phase 14296 selects phase 14288 and value 16054. The two steps are +9 and +48, giving X 63032, Z **23875**. Quantizing the heading first would make cosine use phase 14304 and value 16059, wrongly adding 49 to Z.

**Established — whole-record comparison.** The projection matches all 868 ordinary horizontal steps across 858 ticks of the four replays, including all 109 steps with nonzero speed and a non-cardinal heading. The post-tick records matched the published fixtures. `travel` provides a cardinal control, not evidence of oblique angles. In `movement` tick 90 both the airborne step and the new Run step are oblique, and tick 91 has a post-step Z correction while starting backward slide. Comparing only the net post-collision displacement on those ticks would not isolate the projection.

### Contact with an ordinary door leaf (Q-INT-03)

**Established statically — bound and touch.** Use the nearest authored key of the leaf and the nearest authored body key of Lara, using the earlier one on a tie. Their world Y spans must overlap strictly. In the leaf's frame, Lara's origin must lie inside the nearest key's X and Z bounds expanded by the current collision radius (100 in ordinary dry movement), with equality allowed. The mesh spheres use those nearest keys as well. A leaf mesh sets its own bit when it and any Lara mesh both have positive radii and the squared distance between their centres is strictly less than the square of their summed radii. Store that mask on the leaf, not on Lara; it is not a fixed constant and it replaces, rather than accumulates with, an earlier mask. If the broad bounds pass but the spheres miss, the new mask is zero. If the broad bounds fail, the sphere test does not replace the mask; an active leaf clears the mask at its ordinary animation advance, before Lara can contact it that tick. An unanimated leaf is not guaranteed a new mask on each out-of-range tick.

**Established statically — displacement.** With push permitted and a nonzero mask, move Lara in the leaf's local X/Z to the nearest face of its radius-expanded rectangle, exactly onto the face and with no fixed kick: lower X, higher X, higher Z, then lower Z break ties in that order. The perpendicular coordinate and Y stay unchanged in the leaf's frame. Both transforms use the door yaw and the usual signed 14-bit trig products. Check terrain at the proposed point, facing from Lara's pre-step position toward that point; a blocked result restores the pre-step X/Z, not merely the position just before the push. The check allows a front drop up to 32512, a front rise up to 384, and no front ceiling overlap, with a 762-high body. An accepted push makes the resulting point the new pre-step reference for the rest of the contact update and refreshes Lara's room at 10 above the origin.

**Established statically — bump and direction.** A nonzero mask is not by itself damage or a stun countdown. If the leaf's current and requested states differ, the current movement permits bump reactions, and the leaf's nearest bound is taller than 256, a push supplies a directional bump even if terrain rejects the proposed displacement. Take the bearing from the unexpanded leaf bounds' horizontal midpoint to Lara's position before the push; subtract it from Lara's yaw, add 40960 modulo 65536 and select the resulting quarter-turn. The first such bump plays sound 31. The accumulated hit-frame counter rises by one, capping at 34; it resets if there is no directional bump by the end of the contact pass. This leaf supplies no HP damage and no timed hit countdown. Its current pose and motion, not which authoring room owns the controlled sector, determine the source. `switch-door` checks topology only; physical outcomes here have not been exercised by that fixture.

**Established — three placed controls.** Each record uses Walk+Forward from a fresh stand in The Great Wall. Comparing an opening leaf with and without a pending close request distinguishes animation, sector passability and bump reactions.

### Aiming and resolving a shot

**Established — selecting a candidate.** A candidate must be alive in one of the five active ordinary creature slots. Its origin must be strictly within 8192 of Lara's sight origin (650 above her feet); preliminary per-axis bounds include equality. Sight ends at the horizontal centre of its animated bounds, rotated with it, and one third down vertically. Before any closeness comparison the candidate must be visible and within the acquisition cone relative to body plus torso: yaw ±10920 and pitch ±10920 for paired guns or ±10010 for shotgun, inclusive. An out-of-cone candidate does not compete even at close range. An admitted candidate must be strictly closer and its absolute yaw less than the prior candidate's plus 2730 to replace it. Range ties keep the prior candidate.

| Aim condition | Established result |
|---|---|
| Acquire arm lock | **Established, inherited cone.** Arms check the selected target relative to Lara's body, not the body-plus-torso view used for selection. Clear sight within the same cone locks both arms. A candidate may qualify from the torso view without either arm acquiring. With paired pistols, holding Action keeps a chosen target even if the arms lose lock, or if an ordinary corpse leaves the active list. Releasing Action discards the choice and searches for a living target again. See [the measured lock and corpse sequence](tr2-lara-arms.md#pistol-lock-and-creature-strikes). |
| Retain an arm lock | **Established, inherited limits.** An already locked left arm can keep yaw −30940…10920, and the right −10920…30940, with pitch ±14560. Shotgun retention is yaw ±14560 and pitch ±11830. Endpoints are inclusive. Outside the acquisition cone these ranges can retain but cannot create the lock. Sight loss drops both locks without necessarily changing the choice. One off-angle arm can lower while the other remains locked. |
| Movement of the aim | **Established, inherited rate.** Each arm moves by up to 1820 per tick toward the lock or toward neutral when unlocked. With one locked arm the pistol torso shows half that arm's aim; with two it shows one quarter of their sum. The shaft of the shotgun uses its aimed arm angle, not the average across two competing arm locks. The retained and unretained arms show why the target itself is not an instant arm angle. |
| Shot at a creature | **Established.** Handguns shoot from 650 above the feet and shotgun from 500. On the selected target, qualifying spheres must have each perpendicular offset strictly less than their radius, squared perpendicular distance no greater than radius squared, and forward centre distance strictly greater than the radius. The smallest qualifying forward centre distance less radius wins. The hit subtracts damage, marks the target hit, makes blood and counts a kill only when crossing from positive HP to zero or less. An unrelated creature on the ray is not substituted. Later monks can track retaliation, not an early wildlife damage multiplier. |
| No target, blocked lock, or miss | **Established.** Without a target, Action can shoot forward but cannot hit a creature sphere. A selected target without a lock prevents that arm from discharging. On an issued ray with no qualifying sphere, the range-8192 miss is limited by the world and may ricochet. A closed door or ordinary wall cannot shatter like type 72 glass. |
| Pane with or without a creature hit | **Established.** An issued shot can shatter an intact pane without hitting a creature, or do both. A direct hit uses the sphere endpoint without the additional world cutoff that applies to a miss. Both compare [the pane's own bounds in the shot's rooms](tr2-venice-objects.md#glass-gondolas-and-aquatic-mines); pane contact is not another damage roll or a rule that glass absorbs a bullet. Sight obstruction at lock acquisition is separate. |
