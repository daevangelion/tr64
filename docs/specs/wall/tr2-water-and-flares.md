Game: Tomb Raider II, Windows PC Steam release (appid 225300).
Executable: `Tomb2.exe`, 912896 bytes; SHA-256 `fd9c91d14d54540b3e00c9bedafeef9e4ddb1e9542adc9f29170c7f19b1a657b`.
Inputs: retail executable and authored PC levels The Great Wall (`WALL.TR2`) and Venice (`BOAT.TR2`); I baseline on tr64 `cdfb71d`.
Reader: WallRE2. Date: 2026-11-21. Evidence: static retail comparison, authored clips and saved-state behaviour; no runtime or hardware lighting comparison.

Y increases down; depth at the feet means origin Y minus water height, while column depth means floor height minus water height. A sector is 1024 wide. Times are logic ticks (30 per second) and angles use 65,536 per turn. Compare [I's water rules](lara-and-enemies.md#131-water-transitions-and-laras-pitch-and-roll-amendment) and [I's current](triggers-and-effects.md#7-sink-code-2-underwater-current).

## Water status before movement

**Established — depth and boundary.** For the waterline, first follow horizontal room links at Lara's X/Z, then look up from a water sector or down from an air sector for the water/air boundary. From below use the ceiling click of the highest water sector; from above use the floor click of the air sector immediately before the water. The line height is that signed click times 256, not itself a slope sample. Column depth is the live floor at Lara's position, including slopes and raised floors, minus the waterline. A water room with no link to air above is treated as deep. The trace's signed `waterDistance` is line Y minus Lara's Y or 32512 if no line is found. It is sampled before movement and not rewritten to reflect a pose or position change later that tick; mode can already have changed in the same sample. Vehicle and scripted pose exceptions skip the ordinary mode change.

### Continuing poses in shallow water (Q-T2W-07)

**Established — backward walk.** While alive and in state 16, hold Back to keep goal 16 in wading mode. Walk neither enables it nor keeps it going on its own. Back release or nonpositive HP requests Stand (2) immediately; the pose still needs its authored transition. The water-mode test runs before this decision. In `wade-step` 39–70, Back alone keeps goal 16, including the first continuing update at 40; on 71 Back release requests goal 2 while clip 61 still advances. **Established statically — other Walk conditions.** A retained forward Walk or eligible Run with Forward in wading mode requests Wade (65) whether Walk is held or not; their earlier death, Roll or jump decisions are unchanged where those decisions already take priority. Without Forward they request a stop as on dry ground. Continuing Wade itself holds on Forward alone, asks for Stand on release, and asks for Run with Forward on the first dry update, regardless of Walk on that update. A returning dry Run distinguishes Walk again on its own controls. A left or right turn with Forward chooses Wade rather than reading Walk to choose a gait; Walk still limits the turn instead of asking for fast turn once the ordinary turn rate is exceeded, unless weapon readiness has already requested fast turn. ### Collision envelopes (Q-T2W-02)

**Established statically — how to read the limits.** These are the forward and side floor limits of the normal room probe, not a guarantee that Lara follows a drop by the allowed amount. With Y down, the **positive** limit is the largest admitted drop below the probe's foot reference; the **negative** limit is the greatest admitted rise, expressed as a negative difference. A rise or drop at the stated limit is admitted by the floor comparison. `32512` is the effectively open downward limit in the same convention as I; the separate missing-floor and enclosure tests still apply. Front ceiling Y minus the top of the body must be **strictly below** the ceiling limit. A zero limit thus rejects a front ceiling exactly at the top. The common probe finds linked sectors at a point 160 above the body's top; it still measures floor from the foot reference and ceiling from the top.

**Established — shared horizontal layout.** Wading, surface and submerged collision use the ordinary centre, front and two leading-corner samples at the radius in the table. Choose the nearest cardinal to the collision-time travel heading, with ties toward increasing yaw. Start at proposed X/Z. The front sample moves one full radius on the signed cardinal axis; its transverse offset is radius times the heading's signed sine for an X offset or cosine for a Z offset, rounded down at the 14-bit trig scale. Each corner is also one full radius along the cardinal axis, but exactly one radius to its left or right. Thus for facing +Z the corners are (−radius, +radius) and (+radius, +radius) in X/Z, not a triangle of heading-rotated probes. All four find sector membership at the lookup height above and measure heights from their common body references. Use the wading pose's dry-style heading (for example yaw plus half a turn for backward walk), the surface stroke's selected heading, or the underwater heading chosen before contact; an underwater side impact changes yaw only after these probes. The layout is established statically; the bank and underwater correction samples below exercise it without distinguishing every corner.

**Established — water/weapon interlock.** Surface or submerged status does not allow drawing an ordinary dry weapon; a ready dry weapon is put away there. The harpoon and flare are distinct exceptions. Wading permits ordinary drawing at less than 650 feet depth for pistol types and less than 500 for the other ordinary guns. Already ready, they are put away above their respective depth, not at equality. Temperature, visibility and the swimming pitch aren't tests for using the flare. Compare the same wet movement with a harpoon/flare and with an ordinary firearm, and compare the draw and stow sides at each equality boundary.

## Numbered animation effects and bubbles (Q-T2A-05)

**Established statically — cue admission.** A frame effect (animation command kind 6) applies at its exact authored frame after ordinary animation transition selection, before the animation's speed displacement. The low 14 bits identify the effect. For Lara's body, high bits both clear mean no restriction. The `16384` flag alone permits the cue at nonnegative pre-movement `waterDistance`, or at the exceptional value −32512; the `32768` flag alone requires negative `waterDistance`. Both flags together refuse the cue. Ordinary world-item animation instead uses just the effect number at the matching frame. These conditions are separate from animation **sound** cues. The Great Wall's 26 Lara body bubble cues are all unrestricted; a cue can therefore emit bubbles without testing current water mode, room wetness, air or health.

**Established statically — effect 3 emission.** Use the **presentation** stream, the second `rng` state in the [trace](tr2-oracle-trace.md#record-fields). It has the same 32-bit advance and 15-bit result as the [gameplay stream](tr2-lara-arms.md#pistol-attempts-and-random-draws-q-t2a-01), but a separate state. Draw once and multiply the result by three, dividing by 32768 and rounding down. Zero bubbles means no other action. With one or two, request sound 37 at the actor's origin with the underwater sound mode. Then transform local (0, 0, 50) at the actor's animated head joint (joint 14), and request that many transient type-231 bubble effects in the actor's stored room. These occupy effect slots, not item slots, and do not appear in the public active-item trace. Each successfully allocated bubble takes two more presentation draws in order: speed is 10 plus the first result times six divided by 32768; sprite frame is the negative of the second times three divided by 32768. Round the nonnegative quotients down, giving speeds 10–15 and sprite frames 0, −1 or −2. An allocation failure does not draw those two numbers. The sound request precedes allocation and can add presentation draws for sound settings, not gameplay draws. In `WALL.TR2` sound 37 has one sample, no chance roll, and random volume then pitch if audible; the pitch draw only occurs if the resulting volume is positive. An earlier sound rejection (including a dry camera room) can skip both.

**Established statically — effect 3 lifetime.** No fixed tick countdown. A bubble first moves in the next effect update after its creation. Each update takes Y up by its initial speed. Advance the horizontal phases by 1638 for the X sine and 2366 for the Z cosine, then use 11 times sine for X and 8 times cosine for Z, dividing each signed product by 16384 and rounding down. Creation does not reset the slot's phases. Resolve room and ceiling at the new position; retire it if the room is not water, the ceiling is missing, or it has reached or passed the ceiling. Otherwise move it to the new point and linked room. No random draws are needed for this rise or retirement.

**Established — measured stream changes.** In `pool-swim` the tiger first updates on tick 430 while Lara is still dry, with **no bubble cue**. Gameplay RNG changes from 3547461959 to 2860386973: the same first navigation draw pair as in [`tiger-cave`](tr2-great-wall-creatures.md#great-walls-recorded-tiger). A reader-side replay with identical public samples separates the four bubble cues from other draws. It identifies one advance at the end of each preceding rendered frame. **Reader-side evidence only:** that advance belongs to the closing lighting update, which uses the low five bits of the 15-bit result for the next room-light flicker intensity. The camera room is dry at the four cues, so bubble sound consumes no draws. The preceding frame contributes one and the cues contribute 3, 1, 5 and 3 respectively, accounting for the between-sample totals 4, 2, 6 and 4. The published fields check those totals; they carry neither light intensity nor the separate draws.

## Currents

**Established — partly inherited.** The authored sink sets a target box and X/Y/Z position and strength six times its record's signed strength. Unlike I, the position is renewed at the same box number. On surface or submerged movement the prior request pulls Lara toward an admitted waypoint before she makes her own stroke; it uses the flight-style zone allowances (step and drop 20480, vertical rating 256). Each axis independently moves by at most the strength or the remaining distance, as in I. The current displacement is checked for collision before her own stroke. If a waypoint is found that request is spent, and a new sector evaluation can renew it for the next tick. If there is no waypoint, the step is skipped without consuming the request; without a navigation slot there is no pull. Both surface and submerged movement honor that ordering, but the fly cheat does not. This comparison distinguishes the same-box target refresh, the retained physical displacement, and a route where an obstruction prevents the current from moving Lara straight through a wall.

## Flares (new in II)

**Established — stock and pickup.** A box (151) credits six flares; a loose world flare (152) carries its own age. Retrieving it transfers an active or partly spent flare to Lara's left hand at that age, not a new inventory unit. The ordinary ground and underwater mesh poses have different acquisition frames: 58 frames after animation 204 begins (6029), or 20 frames after 206 begins (6098). The submerged flare destination additionally checks a floor within 512 vertically and can catch early at less than 128 remaining distance. The separate stock pickup and [loose-item commitment](tr2-triggers-and-interaction.md#pickups) corroborate the difference.

### Light and in-world flight

**Established — age and visible flame.** The light is eligible at ages 0 through 1799, including submerged or after release. A held flare checks its light before ageing; a loose one advances age before checking light. Compare the same age in hand and in the world: both use the bands below, but draw their light from different points, the held local point (11,32,41) on her left hand or the loose object's position. Light in X is jittered 0–15. Flame visibility is separate from illumination.

**Established — observed presentation counts, not effect attribution.** In `level-finish`, the recorded presentation random state advances zero times on tick 1, once per tick from 2 through 39, twice on the ignition tick 40, and three times per subsequent tick through 122 except for six advances on tick 120. Thus the first lit tick has one advance above the pre-ignition cadence, the later samples ordinarily have two, and tick 120 has three more than that lit cadence. The gameplay random state is unchanged throughout. These are counts recovered from consecutive public samples; those values alone do not establish which updates consume the additional draws.

**Established — falloff.** The room vertices and nearby objects both use the field, not the drawn mesh. For radius 2048, the loss from the peak is squared distance divided by 2048 squared times the peak, rounded down; brightness thus declines to zero at the boundary. At the same distance within 32, a late flicker can instead give the 32-unit field: its integer loss is zero throughout that radius, including at its edge. This field still lights geometry while the flame mesh is dark; at a distance beyond 32 it does not light the same geometry. Neither field changes collision. These comparisons hold at the same flare age; the field and the mesh are not evidence of each other's brightness. Required photographic parity in a particular dark room is not established.

**Established — air versus water.** Horizontal movement uses the old horizontal speed; each signed trigonometric product is rounded down. In air descent speed increases by 6. Underwater both speed components are pulled halfway toward 5, rounding the change toward zero. Vertical movement uses the new descent speed. With nonzero descent at the beginning of the tick pitch adds 546 and roll 910; otherwise both level. A held flare can play sound 12 and make underwater bubbles through the lit interval, even during a flicker blackout. For a loose flare those effects require a visible flame that tick; the underwater bubble chance is 16384 out of 32768. This contrasts the same flare age in air and water, and flame visible and hidden at the same late age. Water does not extinguish the flare.

**Established — saves on this Steam PC release.** A held flare retains its age and hand pose.
