Game and release: Tomb Raider II, Steam appid 225300, Windows PC `Tomb2.exe`, 912896 bytes, SHA-256 `fd9c91d14d54540b3e00c9bedafeef9e4ddb1e9542adc9f29170c7f19b1a657b`.
Reader: WallRE2. Date: 2026-11-21. Inputs: executable, `BOAT.TR2`, and the Tomb Raider I wall baseline at `cdfb71d`.
Evidence: static PC rules, the authored creature poses, and positive and negative placement comparisons. No runtime combat, reload test or PlayStation parity asserted.

One tile is 1024 units; a tick is 1/30 s and one turn is 65536 angular units. Creature distance below is the horizontal distance from its usual forward pivot toward Lara, unless stated otherwise. “Ahead” is strictly within 90°; “bite position” also requires a living target within 384 vertically. See [the shared creature rules](tr2-great-wall-creatures.md#creature-rules-applying-to-these-encounters) for slots, navigation and reaction, and [early firearms](tr2-lara-ground-and-weapons.md#pistols-automatic-pistols-uzis-and-shotgun) for Lara's aiming and damage.

## Gunman aim and shots

| Situation | PC behaviour and cross-check |
|---|---|
| Target eligible for pointing or firing | **Established.** Lara must be alive, ahead and strictly under 8192 horizontally. The shot examines a clear line through the rooms from 768 above the shooter's origin to 768 above Lara's origin. The comparison at exactly 8192 rejects her; a close obstructed Lara and a close clear one do not give the same aim choice. The gunman's aim follows Lara's angle while she is ahead; the model can still have aim at release when the separate shot test fails. |
| Walk, idle and station choice | **Established.** Walk (1) in escape requests Run (2) before testing for a shot. Otherwise a viable shot sends it through idle (3) to aim 6 or 10 with equal chance, also used in waiting state 4. If walking with no shot and Lara ahead at most 2048 away, bored mood allows an idle request on a roll below 768 out of 32768; without that ahead/range case the gunman instead requests running. In wait 4, escape takes priority over the shot or idle changes. With no viable shot, bored and ahead, a roll 0–1279 queues crouch 5 via 3, 1280–2559 queues walk 1 via 3, and a higher roll keeps waiting. Without that bored/ahead case, it uses idle and chooses walk if bored or under 2048, else run. Clips distinguish the destinations, not their random cutoffs. |
| Run and moving fire | **Established.** Escape with a viable shot requests moving fire (9); without one it keeps running. Outside escape, a viable shot under 2048 requests idle (3), regardless of zone. At 2048 or farther a viable shot requests 9 in the same zone and idle otherwise. With no viable shot, bored chooses idle and other moods keep running. In state 9 it attempts a shot only when no continuation is already queued, then queues 9 to stop repeats. The attempt with lost viability requests run; a viable shot that merely misses does not. Clips 331 and 346 contain the moving-fire states; the close, far and obstructed cases establish the choice. |
| Stationary aim prioritizes escape | **Established.** Crouch 5 with a viable shot goes through idle to aim 6. Aim 6 and 10 clear the one-shot latch and request fire 7 or 11 only with a viable target and no escape; otherwise they return toward idle. A fire state attempts once on its first unlatched tick and latches even if the attempt misses. Compare entry at frame 352 of 335 or frame 487 of 343 with the authored sound at 489: the latter does not time the actual shot. |
| Stationary, sideways and wrapped lead | **Established.** Consider viable, clear targets at distance 1024. Stationary Lara has no lead penalty and receives a hit roll, as does a moving Lara whose signed sideways speed in the lead test is 64: the scaled lead wraps to zero. A sideways value of 4 instead produces lead 894784; its square plus distance squared wraps to positive 1775538176, above the limit, so the shot misses without a hit roll. The sideways value is Lara's speed times the sine of her facing relative to the measured bearing, rounded down. Its large scale of 67108864 wraps as a signed 32-bit quantity **before** division by 300 toward zero. The squared lead plus original squared distance wraps again as signed 32-bit. Values at or below 8192 squared admit the roll, not just small positive values. These are consequences of the finite retail arithmetic, not predicted positions or a monotonic penalty for lateral speed. |
| Clear hit roll, miss and obstruction | **Established.** If the shot is viable and the lead admits the roll, a random integer below 8192 plus the integer quotient of (8192 squared less original squared distance) divided by 3276 hits. At distance 1024, 28355 hits and 28356 misses. A hit costs Lara 50, marks her hit, produces blood at a selected joint and sound 50. A viable miss, including one rejected by lead, can produce a floor ricochet at offsets −256…255; a viable hit or miss can both flash for 3 ticks. With no clear sight, an out-of-range target or one not ahead, no hit or such ricochet occurs. A shot attempt can still check shootable scenery separately, so an eligible pane can break without making an obstructed Lara take the 50. |
| Death | **Established.** At 0 HP or below there is no more aiming or firing; it takes one of two equiprobable death clips, 339 (frames 419–434) or 340 (435–474). It stops colliding and releases cargo on retirement, not simply at the lethal hit. Both clips carry the authored terminal instruction. |

## Carried keys, weapons and ammunition

**Established — cargo assignment.** An ordinary pickup at the same exact X/Y/Z and room as a creature becomes unavailable independently and can be carried with other pickups. A creature or prop at the same point is not itself cargo. If multiple creatures start at the same spot, the first matching creature in authored item order takes the ordinary pickups; they are already absent from the room's loose pickups when the next creature is considered. This order comes from comparing the first and subsequent match after attachment, not from a Venice example with two competing carriers.

**Established — final-room release.** Cargo appears when the normal death clip retires its creature, not when the lethal shot lands. It goes to the corpse's exact final origin and room without a separate door check, floor snap, balcony return or automatic award. A clear-body request does not take released loot back. This qualifies “at death” in [the delta map, line 59](tr2-delta-map.md#creatures) and [the earlier companion, line 113](tr2-first-levels.md#32-venice-creatures-shots-and-drops). Saved cargo position and room are separate from corpse animation and activity. **Inferred:** a displaced sniper may leave the key reachable or obstructed, depending on its new geometry; a save between lethal hit and retirement should continue to withhold the goods until retirement. Neither implies a guaranteed recoverable drop.
