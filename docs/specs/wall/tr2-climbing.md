Executable: `Tomb2.exe`, 912896 bytes; SHA-256 `fd9c91d14d54540b3e00c9bedafeef9e4ddb1e9542adc9f29170c7f19b1a657b`.

**Established — difference from I.** Lara can attach to a marked vertical face, not just stay on a horizontal lip. The new stance, climb up/down and sideways climbs are distinct from I's ordinary ledge hang and shimmy. An unmarked ledge still admits an ordinary ledge hang but does not permit a ladder mount. This distinction is supported by the new face records in both levels and the retained ordinary hang animations alongside the ladder animations.

## Which face Lara can climb

**Established.** Floor entry 6 gives the climbable faces of the evaluated sector. Its four direction marks are in bits 8–11 of the floor word (mask 1, 2, 4 and 8 in the entry's direction value). These are alternatives, not a sequence. For checking the mark, facing rounds to the nearest cardinal direction, with ties toward increasing angle. Lara's actual attachment additionally needs clearance and a tighter facing range. An ordinary floor evaluation without an eligible mark clears the right to climb. A mark by itself neither activates an object nor bypasses collision. Cross-check: +X and −X marks occur in The Great Wall, while Venice also has +Z, −Z and multi-face entries. The observed variety includes distinct single- and multi-face records, rather than treating every mark as a four-way ladder.

| Facing | Direction mark |
|---|---|
| **Established:** 0°, toward +Z | 1 |
| **Established:** 90°, toward +X | 2 |
| **Established:** 180°, toward −Z | 4 |
| **Established:** 270°, toward −X | 8 |

**Established — timing.** The mark available to Lara during movement is the one from ordinary floor evaluation. That evaluation is normally later than her movement in the tick, and it can renew the mark while she is already hanging. The mark is therefore not a substitute for testing the next hand position during the current tick. The order agrees with [floor triggers and interactions](tr2-triggers-and-interaction.md#floor-evaluation); the different outcomes for a marked face with clear and obstructed hand positions establish the separate geometry requirement.

## Clearance and side-to-side agreement

**Established — common geometry.** The normal radius is 100. For stance, up, down and side decisions, the two off-centre hand positions are 220 toward local right and 180 toward local left, at 100 forward; a side move concerns the approached hand. For the discrete jump catch the side offsets are instead 100 in either direction and the centre is included. Each wall-position check compares the space there with the space 2 units farther forward. A regular side or descent check covers a 512-unit vertical span based 512 above the trial origin. A standing attach instead bases that span 700 above her origin; a jump catch uses the current pose's bounding-box height. Right and left samples use the same limits: if they require opposite nonzero vertical shifts, a new vertical step is refused. With two compatible shifts the more restrictive shift is used at stance or attachment. This is established by comparing both hands against the single-hand lateral move and the three-position jump catch.

| Where and what qualifies | Outcome at one hand position |
|---|---|
| **Established:** Lara's side, normal wall check | Missing floor is blocked. Relative to the top of the span, a floor more than 70 above its nominal bottom (span length plus 128 down) or a ceiling more than 70 below the top is blocked. Exactly 70 can be corrected. The permitted corrections cannot demand opposite shifts. These come first, before an apparent opening across the face. |
| **Established:** Across the face, solid surface near the top | A floor at most 70 below the span top, or a solid wall with missing floor, is the close-face case. A positive close floor can demand a downward correction; it conflicts with any prior upward correction, so that position is blocked. Otherwise, no ceiling, a ceiling at or above the close floor, or a ceiling at least 512 below the span top gives continued wall contact. Here the far ceiling is evaluated at the lower end of the span. |
| **Established:** Across the face, ceiling lower down | A ceiling at least 512 below the top permits continuing; from 443 through 511 it permits continuing with an upward correction to make room for 512, but only if moving downward is not already required. For a close face, the at-or-above-floor continuation in the previous row takes precedence. If this upward adjustment is obstructed, a usable hang needs the extra depth in the next row. |
| **Established:** Marginal hang rather than continued wall | A hang needs at least 900 of near-side floor depth below the point 128 beneath the span top. This can qualify in a close-face case without the continuation above or with an obstructed upward adjustment. With a far floor, a ceiling 1–442 below the top gives a hang only with that depth; at or above the span top it must also be less than 70 above and no downward shift may be needed. In the latter case any upward correction is at least enough to meet that ceiling. A marginal hold failing these conditions is blocked. |
| **Established:** Higher step sample, before any top-out | For up only, the reference is 768 above the trial origin. The near ceiling can be up to 70 below the point 256 above that reference. Missing far floor means continued wall. For far floor from the reference through 128 below, a positive difference can require downward correction. A far floor above the reference cannot require that correction. In either case, continuation requires a far ceiling at or above the floor or at least 512 below the reference, when sampled 512 below the reference. Otherwise this position is blocked, not a top-out. |
| **Established:** Higher step sample, far floor more than 128 below | A ceiling at least 512 below the up reference means continued wall even if the floor below it is far down. With a higher ceiling, a gap of at least 763 between floor and ceiling gives an upper platform; a smaller gap blocks Lara. This contrasts the space along the wall below a low ceiling with the open space above a usable lip. Both hands must agree before Lara can pull over. |
