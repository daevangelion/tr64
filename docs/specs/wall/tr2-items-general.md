# Tomb Raider II: placed-item defaults, campfire, helicopter, and starting stock

Game and release: Tomb Raider II, Steam appid 225300, Windows PC `Tomb2.exe`, 912896 bytes, SHA-256 `fd9c91d14d54540b3e00c9bedafeef9e4ddb1e9542adc9f29170c7f19b1a657b`.
Reader: WallRE2. Date: 2026-11-22. Evidence: retail rules compared with `WALL.TR2` item and animation records, `TOMBPC.DAT` (SHA-256 `d1f1a9c8f784301824ed6c59c8b34378d84e7f7fc93c90f5df7c4a5c8ed9868c`), and the PC [`stand` and `travel` fixtures](tr2-oracle-trace.md#observed-routes). Y increases downward; a tile is 1024 units, a height click is 256, and a gameplay second contains 30 ticks.

## Ordinary placed-item initialization (Q-T2I-01)

| Property | Established rule and check |
|---|---|
| Identity and authored pose | Keep the placed item slot, type, X/Y/Z, room and facing. Pitch and roll start at zero. Use the **animation base authored for that object model**, its first authored frame, and the clip's authored state for both current and desired state. A type-specific start rule may replace these defaults. For example, the placement for the campfire has no animation override, whereas the [player start](tr2-lara-start.md#lara-before-the-first-gameplay-update) chooses a stance from its room. |
| Health | A model's health defaults to −16384, the inherited untargetable sentinel, unless its type establishes a different value. It is not damage dealt to the object, a death animation, or a signal to remove the item. Two active non-creature examples, the campfire and helicopter, have it from first appearance; Lara and a live tiger have positive health and use the separate damage and targeting rules. Do not assign the sentinel solely by testing whether an object is a creature; the type can override it. |
| Motion and visibility | Ordinary initial motion has zero horizontal and downward speed, no gravity or recent hit, zero queued state and timer, zero touch mask, and all visible-mesh bits set. Generic body contact starts enabled, independently of whether the object supplies a collision response. Seed `roomFloor` from the placed room's own raw authored sector floor click, without following links or evaluating slope. None of these defaults makes a nonmoving item track terrain automatically. |
| Initial activity and flags | Ordinary placements are inactive until awakened. A placed hidden flag (bit 8) is consumed as initial invisible status instead of retaining an initial one-shot lock. Some creature types also begin invisible without that placement flag. A placed cleanup flag (bit 15) is consumed as eligibility for later cleanup, not an initial retirement. An authored full five-bit activation mask is cleared and the reverse-start bit (14) is set. A full-mask item with a continuing behaviour starts in the update set, with later such placements ahead of earlier ones. An item awakened later by Lara appears **before** already-active items in that tick's sample, before its first update on the following tick. Compare the initially active campfire and door with the later helicopter and tiger wakes. These defaults do not establish [the tiger's health and behaviour](tr2-great-wall-creatures.md), [the door's opening rule](tr2-lara-ground-and-weapons.md#ordinary-doors-and-timed-closing), or [door 72's startup sector changes](tr2-oracle-trace.md#record-fields). See [shared activation](tr2-triggers-and-interaction.md#item-masks-timers-and-action-ordering). |

## Campfire, slot 100 (object type 253)

| Situation | Established retail rule and comparison |
|---|---|
| Starting awake | The campfire in room 65 at (85504, 20480, 68096) starts with all five authored activation bits, so it starts reverse and ticking, with no activation bits left in the sampled flags. Because a reverse item with an incomplete mask is triggered, it does not need a player standing nearby. No floor action in the level names this campfire as an activation recipient; the same placement scan finds activation actions for the nearby-level helicopter and pool-bank tiger as positive controls. This is distinct from other maps requiring a trigger before a flame is spawned. |
| Emitter versus flame | The records show the unchanged campfire in model clip 386, frame 0, current and desired state 0, health −16384. **Established statically:** on a triggered tick without a flame it starts one separate stationary effect at its origin and room, with sprite frame and cooldown zero. If the effect pool is full, it can try again next tick. It does not animate or move the item. While untriggered it removes any flame it made and remembers none. I also separates emitter and flame. The records do not sample the separate effect. |
| Scenery flame and proximity | **Established statically.** The separate flame cycles its sprite and plays sound 150 at its origin. A positive cooldown decreases by one and prevents damage on that tick. At zero, both absolute horizontal components must be at most 600, horizontal squared distance must be at most 360000, and the flame's Y minus Lara's origin Y must be in −3072…3072. That difference must also lie between Lara's interpolated top bound and her bottom bound plus 100, inclusive. This is a horizontal disk and vertical pose test, not a spherical 600-unit radius or a same-room test. |
| Damage and ignition | **Established statically.** A qualifying scenery-flame tick costs 5 HP and marks Lara hit, versus 3 HP in I. It additionally tries to ignite Lara if the horizontal distance is strictly less than 450; exactly 450 can take ordinary proximity damage but does not ignite. The scenery flame sets its cooldown to 100 on that close hit, even if Lara is already burning or no new attachment can be made. Ordinary scenery fire does not attach a second flame while Lara is already burning; the [separate true-floor lava rule](tr2-delta-map.md#lara-health-and-water) can attach up to ten effects. An attached flame moves with Lara, deals 7 HP and marks a hit per burn tick, versus 5 in I; it goes out if its Y is strictly below a defined water surface. None of the sampled routes approaches this fire. |

## Helicopter, slot 101 (object type 259; Q-T2I-02)

**Established — first active tick.** The normal departure triggers in room 36 make the helicopter appear in the active list after Lara's movement. Its first movement is on the following tick, as in `travel` 204–205. Its placement in room 69 supplies the initial raw floor click −20 (−5120). The model supplies clip 388, initially frame 0, state and goal 0. It starts with no gravity or authored animation translation.

| Part of an active update | Established effect |
|---|---|
| Position and sound | It first moves exactly +100 world Z, independently of facing or recorded speed. Sound 297 is requested at the point one quarter of the way from Lara to the moved helicopter, each signed difference divided by four and rounded down separately. It sees Lara's position at the start of the world-item pass, before her movement on that tick. |
| Retirement | After the +100 and sound, retire it when the absolute difference between that new helicopter Z and Lara's then-current Z is strictly greater than 30720. `pool-swim` first omits it at tick 616, when the move would place it at Z 67460 and separation 30813. `pool-wade` first omits it at tick 603, with predicted Z 66160 and separation 30734. **Established statically, not distinguished by the traces:** either sign can cause retirement; equality does not; X/Y are irrelevant. The placed item is marked retired and removed from activity and from its current room's items, but that last update still finishes its animation and room check. |
| Animation and room | The ordinary item animation advances one frame before the [linked-sector room check](tr2-oracle-trace.md#record-fields), which uses the moved origin starting from the live room. A room is not chosen simply by checking its authored bounds; a blocked outward transfer can leave the helicopter in room 72 after it flies beyond the room's extent. Clip 388 runs from frame 0 through 13 and loops to 0. Its speed and acceleration are zero, and it has no movement commands: animation keeps the horizontal field at zero, and nongravity animation leaves the vertical field alone. Neither field is set to the direct 100-unit drift. Updating room membership does not refresh `roomFloor`. In `travel` tick 205 Z is 28260, room 71 and frame 1, with both speed fields zero and `roomFloor` still −5120. |

## Where The Great Wall's starting inventory comes from (Q-T2I-03)

| Source | Established responsibility |
|---|---|
| Retail game rules for a new campaign | For the first campaign level the game establishes pistols selected and holstered, a shotgun with 12 pellet units (two shells), two flares, one small and one large medikit. The live pistol reserve starts at 1000 and is unlimited in use; see [the startup equipment rule](tr2-lara-start.md#lara-before-the-first-gameplay-update). This stock is a built-in first-level rule, not a `TOMBPC.DAT` grant or a collection of pickups placed at the spawn. The script's absence of a start grant here contrasts with its positive start-grant entry in Tibetan Foothills. |
| `TOMBPC.DAT`: selection and progression | Campaign slot 1's playable name and file are The Great Wall and `WALL.TR2`. Its campaign sequence selects the Modern entry movie (slot 2), music track 33 for play, and the playable level slot 1. After completion it selects music track 3, cut angle zero and cut slot 0 (`CUT1.TR2`), then level statistics and ordinary progression to the next slot. The file also supplies the level's displayed key names Guardhouse Key and Rusty Key, and the global secret track 47. The exact [finish and carry](tr2-great-wall-level.md#exit-cut-and-venice-carry) are separate from the floor trigger that ends play. |
| `TOMBPC.DAT`: grant class | The campaign item-grant event (kind 18) distinguishes operands below 1000, which are rewards for completing the secret set, from operands of 1000 or higher, which are start grants. An entry adds one of its named item and repeated entries add multiple quantities. The Great Wall has four **reward** entries only: operand 6 grenade launcher, 13 grenade ammunition (twice), and 15 small medikit. They do not enter the fresh stock. Tibetan Foothills has a start-class operand 1022 alongside separate reward entries; that confirms both classes exist in the same script format. The Great Wall has no strip, forced opening pose, or authored replacement-floor setting in its sequence. |
| `WALL.TR2`: placement and local timing | Lara's location, rooms, camera placements and local trigger actions are level data, not campaign stock or script definitions. In particular, the helicopter's departure and the first campfire's reverse start are authored in the level; the dragon chime and final secret reward are campaign data, while dragon positions are level data. |
