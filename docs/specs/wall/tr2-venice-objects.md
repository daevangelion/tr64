# Tomb Raider II: Venice vehicles, glass, lock and race

Game and release: Tomb Raider II, Steam appid 225300, Windows PC `Tomb2.exe`, 912896 bytes, SHA-256 `fd9c91d14d54540b3e00c9bedafeef9e4ddb1e9542adc9f29170c7f19b1a657b`.
Reader: WallRE2. Date: 2026-11-21. Inputs: pinned executable, `BOAT.TR2`, and Tomb Raider I wall specifications at `cdfb71d`.
Evidence: static retail PC rules, set against the authored Venice object types, animations, sectors and alternate rooms. No runtime capture, reload test or PlayStation parity asserted.

Distances are in game units (1024 per tile); Y increases downward. Angle units are 65536 per turn. Each tick is 1/30 s. See [shared triggers and interactions](tr2-triggers-and-interaction.md) for switch and camera eligibility and [ordinary doors](tr2-lara-ground-and-weapons.md#ordinary-doors-and-timed-closing) for leaf contact versus portal closure. A speedboat controlled by the player, a movable empty hull and a fixed gondola are distinct: I's boat imagery was not a driveable vehicle with these rules.

## Riding and driving the speedboat (14)

| Situation | What the PC release does |
|---|---|
| Common boarding conditions | **Established — contact.** Normal contact processing requires positive HP. Lara must not already ride or have occupied hands. She may be up to 200 ahead along the hull's local axis, but animated bounds widened by her radius must also overlap and the spheres touch. If there is contact but no boarding variant, the hull remains solid. This separates forward reach from contact and pose tests. |
| Surface or waist-deep boarding | **Established — admission.** Lara must not be under gravity and the hull must have zero horizontal speed. Hold Action; Forward is not tested and is only a way to approach the hull. For boat yaw minus Lara yaw, +8193…24575 admits one side and −24575…−8193 the other; ±8192 and ±24576 fail. Submerged swimming does not admit either. **Established — pose.** The positive range enters clip 267 (353–452), negative enters 259 (0–99). The angles come from the comparison, not the clips. This qualifies the blanket side-Action account in [the earlier companion, line 117](tr2-first-levels.md#33-speedboat-14-and-gondola-24). |
| Boarding while dry | **Established — admission and pose.** Facing difference must be strictly within ±24576. With positive descent, Lara boards only if the hull origin is above her origin, using clip 265 (289–327). With zero descent and normal overlap, a different position also uses 265; exact X/Y/Z coincidence starts directly in seating clip 260. Negative descent does not board. Action is not required in any of these dry cases, unlike side boarding at the surface. The clips corroborate ordinary mount and direct seating, not the positional inequality. |
| Attachment | **Established.** Boarding wakes a dormant boat and initially puts Lara in its room at hull X/Z and 5 above its origin, upright and facing with it, with zero gravity and speed. She does not throttle during a mount. While attached she follows the hull's position, orientation and room, and a living rider's animation poses the hull. The placement follows the snap independently of the clips that return it to sitting. |
| Forward, slow and boost | **Established.** With throttle available, Back wins even if Forward is held. Otherwise Forward selects limit 90, Walk with Forward selects 30, and Action with Forward selects 140 even with Walk. Below the selected limit gain 2 plus five times signed speed divided by twice that limit, truncating the fraction toward zero. Thus an accelerating step can overshoot. At the limit or one above, speed holds; more than one above, it drops by 1. Compare the same incoming speed under the three forward modes and with Back. |
| Brake or reverse | **Established.** Back subtracts 5 at positive speed or at any speed down to −19 inclusive. At −20 or below it keeps the speed. At zero it begins reverse at −5; it does not stall there. Depending on the first speed, the last reverse step can pass −20, subject to subsequent collision correction. |
| Neither Forward nor Back, or empty boat | **Established.** Without Fore/Back, a steering input makes nonnegative speeds 0–19 become 20, overriding coast but not a throttle command. At 20 or more it coasts normally, losing 1; with no steering, speeds below 2 become zero. Negative speed also becomes zero if coasting rather than backing. An awake empty or dead-rider hull coasts by 1 at speeds 2 and up and otherwise goes to zero; dismount need not instantly stop a moving hull. |
| Steering and tick order | **Established.** Left alone steers negative and Right alone positive; Back reverses that sense. Normal PC input cancels opposed Left and Right before boat control, so neither steers or supplies the speed-20 nudge. Turn grows by 22 to magnitude 728 in the same sense or changes by 45 toward an opposite request; absent a command it relaxes 45 toward zero. Hull movement uses the speed and turn from before this tick's throttle and steer input, with prior collision deflection added to its yaw. The steer bank is six times the turn. |
| Jump, Look, water height and ride poses | **Established.** No throttle while the centre has no waterline or is more than 128 above it; at exactly 128 controls can still drive. Jump or Look suppresses throttle without applying coast braking to a normally controllable rider. Look can aim upward when stopped. Jump can separately ask to exit even while Look is held; neither key bypasses the higher-priority death, falling or collision pose. Mount and dismount poses admit no driving. In seat 1, positive speed can override a new exit request in favour of moving state 2. The death pose is 8 (277); a hull more than 128 above its target and already descending can take airborne pose 6 (274). Clips corroborate pose, not which request wins. |

## Hull motion, collision and departure

| Situation | What the PC release does |
|---|---|
| Structural samples and clear travel | **Established.** Sample both sides 300 out, at 750 toward the bow or stern, plus the separate bow point 1000 ahead. Pitch and roll affect the sample heights. Each of these five offset samples uses water height if available, otherwise floor, minus 5 in either case; if neither is valid it returns −32512 unchanged. The horizontal centre-rise test instead takes raw water or raw fallback floor without the 5-unit offset. The final centre suspension has its own rule below. The forward side points and centre have separate suspension; the rear and extra bow samples do not. Unobstructed travel retains the intended movement, with roll and pitch drift from sine scaled by 30 and 10. A nonzero angle that rounds to zero still causes one unit of drift. |
| Blocked sample or bow omission | **Established.** A newly sampled target more than 128 higher than that point's old height or old support, whichever was higher, makes it obstructed. At exactly 128 it does not. The four side points are always tested; the extra 1000-unit bow obstruction test is used only at pitch exactly zero. Even the smallest nonzero pitch gives signed fore/aft drift and disables that one test; the separate centre rise test (strictly over 128 from current hull height) remains. |
| Same-tile, straight or diagonal correction | **Established.** A blocked point in the same tile takes back its attempted horizontal move, contributing no turn. A one-axis crossing puts that point back on the old side of the boundary. For a diagonal crossing, each intermediate side is blocked only when its floor is strictly more than 256 above the old sample. One blocked side gives a one-axis correction; two blocked sides correct both axes without contributing a turn; if neither side is blocked, both attempted components are taken back with no turn. The four corner points contribute turns according to the direction of their one-axis correction, as below. The extra bow and centre can correct position but do not contribute to the turn sum. |
| Airborne versus supported at each suspension point | **Established.** While above the target support, move by the old downward speed, adding 6 to it for the next tick unless the move would pass below support. On passing below, snap to the target and stop the fall. At or below support, place the point no lower than the target now and adjust the velocity by one eighth of the difference between the signed support-height error and old velocity, rounding down; upward velocity is capped at 20. This is a reset of position on contact, not the fall acceleration. Pitch follows the centre-to-mean-bow angle and roll the mean bow versus one side, halfway toward the target each tick; a zero target levels residual angles below 4. |
| Other hull or gondola | **Established.** A same-room empty hull pushes this boat away if their horizontal separation is strictly below 1000; a ridden other hull does not. For an idle gondola, the same strict near bound is measured from a point 512 behind its origin. Inside range there are three outcomes: below speed 80, push away at any height; at speed 80 or above and gondola Y minus boat Y below 2048, request shatter without a push; at speed 80 or above and that signed difference 2048 or greater, neither push nor shatter. At horizontal distance exactly 1000 there is no interaction. Coincident centres make the push arithmetic singular; its runtime outcome is not established. |
| Crash, damage and collision pose | **Established.** A terrain correction (or pitch drift) can reduce forward speed; compare projected corrected versus original speed. Only a ridden hull initially at least 96 and losing more than 10 costs the rider half the loss rounded down, with a hit mark and sound 31; at loss 10 there is no injury. Ordinary impacts do not use hull HP. A nonzero correction to the attempted position selects rider state 5 when not already in it, except when death or the falling pose wins. Dominant sideways correction chooses 270 for right or 271 for left; dominant along-axis correction chooses 272 backward or 273 forward. Equal lateral/along magnitudes choose lateral. These clips are authored pose choices; damage does not depend on that clip entering. |
| Jump off right or left | **Established — request and release.** After collision, death and airborne checks, Jump with Right or Left can request state 3 or 4. Opposed directions cancel in the normal input; in idle, positive boat speed can instead request state 2. Release is on the last frame of right clip 266 (352) or left clip 264 (288). In either case Lara faces 90° away from the boat and enters jump 77 with gravity enabled, upward speed 40 and horizontal speed 20, starting 90 above the old seat. **Established — sideways destination.** Sample 360 along that outward heading, at the raised Y, following room links from the rider's room. A floor no more than 346 above the old seat Y allows the displacement and room change, including equality. An obstructing rise or missing floor leaves her above the boat's X/Z instead, still released with gravity and the same launch speeds. The boat goes to its idle pose, without immediately losing its motion. |
| Wake, riding camera and triggers | **Established.** After collision corrections and the rider's acceleration or the empty hull's coast, use the resulting signed speed for all wake tests. With nonzero resulting speed and hull Y exactly 5 above the sampled water plane, the hull tries up to three sprites 700 behind at offsets −300, 0 and +300. They start at hull height with one quarter of that final speed rounded down; the [wake rule](tr2-oracle-trace.md#venice-extension-in-schema-22) gives the shared pool, descent draws and retirement. Riding supplies ordinary camera elevation −3640 and distance 2048, not an override of a permitted fixed view. A ridden hull evaluates the ordinary and then heavy sector at its proposed centre before aligning the rider or completing release. An empty hull does neither while travelling; a mine it sets off can still issue its own heavy action. |
| Saved boat | **Established.** A placed hull stores its pose, motion, animation and its own steering/buoyancy data, and a mounted save identifies the rider's boat. **Inferred:** the saved hull resumes from that location, subject to the next surface and contact checks; this does not imply that every saved combination of rider and terrain has identical first-tick motion. |

**Established statically — centre suspension and no height.** After horizontal motion, the centre floats at water height minus 5, or at the raw floor if there is no water. With neither, the centre uses −32512, not −32517. The stored ordinary floor field is separate: it is always the sampled floor minus 5, including −32517 when that floor is missing. A supported dry bow point can therefore be 5 higher than the dry centre; an offset sample also returns the unchanged sentinel when it has neither water nor floor.

**Established — signed terrain deflection.** Take each attempted corner sample's signed X and Z offsets from the hull centre just before correcting that point. The table gives the yaw contribution in angle units for each one-axis correction, whether the original crossing was straight or diagonal. Add the four corner contributions to the next tick's heading alongside the steering turn. Opposite corrections with equal lever arms cancel; a centred sample has no lever arm for that axis. This contrasts single-axis corrections, where the sign depends on the direction of the push and the sample's side, with two-axis or same-tile restoration, which contributes zero.

| Direction hull is pushed back | Contribution to next yaw |
|---|---|
| +X | **Established:** the sample's signed Z offset. |
| −X | **Established:** the negative of its Z offset. |
| +Z | **Established:** the negative of its X offset. |
| −Z | **Established:** its signed X offset. |

## Glass, gondolas and aquatic mines

| Object and condition | What the PC release does |
|---|---|
| Intact glass, dormant versus activated | **Established.** All ten Venice panes are type 72. Intact glass is solid on bounds and sphere contact and blocks the box where marked blockable. A complete floor mask makes the pane eligible for updates; it does not break untouched glass or itself remove collision. An accepted shot can break an intact pane even while dormant. Compare the partial and full masks in the villa with a pane whose separate contact threshold is reached. |
| Physical breaking test | **Established.** Off the boat, an updating pane needs a registered touch and the magnitude of Lara's speed projected through the pane at least 50. At 49, or without fresh touch, it stays intact; a slow graze may still push. While riding, an updating pane instead checks the pane origin against Lara: horizontal distance at most 512, vertical height inside her animated bounds through 100 past the bottom, also limited to ±3072. It does not compare hull speed. Shots or qualifying grenades are independent from both contact outcomes. |
| What counts as a bullet intersection | **Established.** An unspent type-72 pane can qualify from the shot's rooms even while dormant. Its yaw quadrant determines which horizontal bounds to use. On each face of the dominant horizontal ray axis (X at equality), the crossing must be in the shot direction and strictly inside the height range. Its transverse coordinate may be on or inside the bounds; crossing beyond opposite sides at the two faces also qualifies when both heights are inside. The face test itself does not limit its distance to the ray endpoint. Across rooms the first examined room with a qualifying object wins. In the same room, the most recently placed into that room wins among qualifying panes or the bell, regardless of distance or type. With unchanged fresh placements that means the higher authored item number first; a subsequent room transfer puts the incoming object first. A ray can hit a creature as well as a pane; a miss may instead ricochet. |
| Shattered glass | **Established.** A real break clears the blockable nav box's blocked mark, disables leaf collision and ticking, plays sound 58 and scatters fragments. Once broken, another ordinary shot or sector action does not re-create the intact pane. Reloaded broken panes keep noncollision and show only mesh 8. The restored state of the nav box after a reload is **not established**; that is separate from the saved mesh and collision. |
| Sinking gondola (24) | **Established, new.** A boat's break request from idle state 1 shatters four upper meshes and requests state 2. Sinking starts in state 3 at animation 362, frame 115, descending 50 per tick. At the floor it stops downward motion and asks for state 4. The terminal clip 364 retires it from ticking. A hull avoids only state 1, so the piece ceases to present the same speedboat obstruction before becoming a settled wreck. This distinguishes authored sinking from instantly removing the whole object. |
| Arming Venice's mines | **Established.** There are six placed mines of type 222. Room 97 tiles (26,42–44) give them full mask by ordinary, untimed activation. Once ticking, an unspent mine with no chain yet looks for a type-14 hull in the room found 2048 above it, strictly within horizontal distance 512. It uses that room and distance rather than an additional vertical separation. Swimming alone does not qualify, and the hull can be empty. Type 221 is not a placed or spawned mine; the removed boat briefly takes that type for its breakup before returning to type 14. Compare the actual mine placements with what happens at a ridden hull, an empty hull and a swimmer. |
| First blast and bailout | **Established.** The first qualifying mine shatters and removes that one boat, and evaluates the floor under the mine as a heavy trigger. If Lara is still riding that boat she shatters, goes to zero health and disappears. Someone already off it survives that rider test even within sight of the blast; an empty coasting hull therefore can start the chain. Each blast splashes and plays 105; when an effect can be allocated, a blast sprite appears 1024 above the mine. A spent mine only shows mesh 0 and no longer collides or detonates. These are mine outcomes, not unusually severe boat hull impacts. |
| Subsequent blasts and reloading | **Established.** On each active update of an unspent mine that sees the chain already set, take one 15-bit **gameplay** draw before any blast allocation or effect. Values 30720–32767 detonate (1/16); lower values leave it unspent for a later update. A mine later in the same active order can roll on the first blast's tick; an earlier one has already taken its turn. A spent or dormant mine does not roll. With no chain, checking for the first hull uses no chain-chance draw. Later blasts do not repeat hull or rider destruction and can still spend the mine when no blast slot is available. Fresh level setup, including the setup preceding save restoration, clears the chain; restoration retains each mine's spent flag, spent mesh and activity. **Inferred:** unspent mines wait for another eligible hull after a mid-chain load rather than resuming the random chain. |

## Lock and the clock-tower race

| Event | Established PC outcome and evidence |
|---|---|
| Lower lock switch and flip | **Established.** Lever 48 (104), room 60 (28,33), toggles 39/38 and 53/54 with full mask, then the actual successful flip of slot 0 permits water-rush effect 5. Lower reverse-start leaves are told to close and inner ordinary leaves to open. The swap does not reposition or wake a boat. An awake hull samples the new plane on its next tick and can rise with it; a dormant one remains at its pose until it starts ticking. The water-rush sound (79) can play at ages 0–120 only after a successful swap, from Lara's X/Z and camera aim Y plus 100 times the absolute difference of age and 30. The new geometry, the wake status and the cue are separate effects. |
| Underwater outlet | **Established.** Lever 40 (105) is placed in room 49, but its switch is authored in alternate room 64 at (32,35), appearing in live room 62 after the flip. Its use opens 56 and 55 and requests view 3 for 2 seconds looking at 52. The unflipped room-62 sector has no such switch. This does not flip the water again. |
| Press versus start | **Established.** Race push button 116, type 103 in room 129 (84,59), arms clock item 76 (type 216) and opens gate leaves 74/73 at full mask, in that order around a 2-second camera 7 looking at subject 75. That switch uses timer zero: the gates can open without the clock counting or ringing. A fixed view does not override Look or combat; dismissal does not stop the doors or their later timers. Compare the switch's nonzero camera timer with its untimed door/clock actions. |
| Cross the threshold | **Established.** Room 129 X=78, Z=59–61 are ordinary records with a timer of 24 seconds (720 ticks) and a zero activation mask. They write timers to leaf 73, clock 76 and leaf 74, without giving any new mask bits. A dormant unlatched leaf can therefore retain 720 without waking, and a dormant clock can hold that value without counting. The button is needed for normal open-and-time play. Standing on the threshold refreshes the counts instead of consuming travel time there. A ridden boat can evaluate the same upper cells; going under through a different room need not evaluate them. The difference is the sector record, not a blanket water exception. |
| Bell, expiry and gate animation | **Established.** An active clock sounds 208 at remaining counts 719, 659 and so on through 59, and again at expiry, 13 rings in one uninterrupted run. Sound originates one sixty-fourth of the way from Lara toward the clock. The clock goes inactive and clears its mask on expiry. Each leaf has its own timer; a timer decrement reaching expiry is still active for that tick. After the closing request, leaf 73 enters 431 at frame 2 and leaf 74 enters 435 at frame 1. Both clips retain the open state through 61. Only on a later leaf tick beginning in the closed clip (430 or 434) does it shut the sector. The last ring, visible closing and actual blockage are distinct, not the older route's “about 23 seconds” as an exact portal interval. |
| Alternate opening, reset and saved race | **Established.** Water room 130 X=81, Z=57–61 opens the leaves with timer zero; X=82 in that span has an ordinary anti-trigger for both. Room 146 (24,76)/(25,76) gives a heavy untimed opening. These alter leaves only. The dry timer-only threshold does not rearm a spent clock; the button's mask can. Saved fields retain the clock's activity and counter and the leaves' timers and animation; [cell restoration](tr2-lara-ground-and-weapons.md#ordinary-doors-and-timed-closing) is reconsidered after load. **Inferred:** resuming a saved race should resume from those clock/leaf values, but not necessarily from the same passable portal on the first tick. **Established — finish.** Room 146 X=24–25, Z=77 has an ordinary finish without timer, boat, mine or dragon test. A swimmer or rider reaching it can finish; this does not prove a particular bypass route is traversable. |
